# V2-P05 — Missing Mechanism Variants

Exec plan for `docs/phase-reports/V2-P05.md`, written before this phase's results were read. It
follows `docs/v2/phase-conventions.md`, implements `docs/OMP_UPGRADE_PLAN_V2.md` §6 (V2-P05) and its
prompt, and is scored under the V2-P03 protocol like V2-P04 was.

## Why

V1's card set has one rejected mechanism (M003), one weak one (M004), one whose named link was never
exercised (M005), one with a policy-dependent verdict (M001), one whose gate/weather attribution is
untested outside a single arm (M002), and one that is unidentified because the model has no channel
for it at all (M006). The phase builds the variants that can decide them, and refuses to build the
ones the evidence cannot support.

## Scope

1. **M006 — a mortality interface, not a mortality rule.** The V1 card is `UNIDENTIFIED` and says why:
   the model has no mortality clock, no arm can bind, and no artifact reports the population decline
   the mechanism would explain. Unless the V2-P01 evidence supplies both a structure and a range, the
   phase delivers the *interface* — the observables a rule would consume, a declared `NoMortality`
   implementation, and a gap report naming exactly what is missing — and the status stays
   `UNIDENTIFIED`. No rate is invented, and no arm is dressed up as an ablation of a rule that does
   not exist.
2. **M004 — mediation against accumulation/foreclosure**, as two structures that can be told apart:
   the V1 credit channel (lend, repay, mediate) and an accumulating branch (a loan serviced below its
   obligation for the declared term is foreclosed, land transfers, and the county's visible base
   shrinks). Intermediates recorded: loans issued, repayments, defaults, land transferred, hidden
   land, and the assessable base.
3. **M005 — concentration attributed by component.** Formation, refugee intake, deserter intake,
   merger, split and dissolution each contribute to the largest band's share; the phase records the
   contribution of each from the log, and runs a **positive control** in which the cohesion bar is low
   enough that merges actually fire and a **negative control** in which they cannot. The card's own
   falsifier is the test: if merges fire and the share does not rise, the merger link is decorative.
4. **M003 — persistence and hysteresis, not a restored ratchet.** Three candidate rules that can
   reduce an arrears stock — settlement, remission, and recovery of receipts — behind parameters whose
   neutral values reproduce V1's behaviour. The rejected monotone claim is *not* restored; what is
   measured is whether the stock persists after the shock and whether the candidates change that.
5. **M001/M002 — policy against structure.** A minimal factorial: two extraction policies crossed with
   the structural levers V2-P04 declared, at the declared seeds, so the question "is the verdict
   conditional on the policy or on the structure?" has an answer that is not one arm at a time.
6. **Mass-balance invariants extended** to grain, silver, land, population, labour and military
   strength, each with the declared sources and sinks, so a channel that leaks mass fails a test.

## Non-goals

- No year, place, person or 1644 outcome is hard-coded in any rule; historical information enters only
  through forcing, priors, patterns and comparisons.
- No new calibrated rates: every new parameter is a declared assumption with a card, neutral at the
  V1 behaviour, and enters the V2 sensitivity pool.
- No V2-P06 work: no calibration, no posterior, no window scoring beyond the P04/P05 arms' own report.
- No mechanism is made to pass a hold-out check; the phase reports what each variant does.

## Controls

Every named arm must actually reach its code path: the run's configuration diff, its event counts and
its chain intermediates are compared before and after, and an arm whose declared intervention produces
no difference fails the phase — the detector V2-P04 built, extended with the positive and negative
controls this phase adds (a control that does not fire is as much a failure as a no-op ablation).

## Acceptance

- Each named arm triggers its event or state path, positive and negative controls behave as declared,
  and no arm is a no-op.
- No rule encodes a year, a place, a person or the 1644 outcome.
- Every intermediate enters the event log and the artifact schema, and a document is recomputable from
  a run's own artifacts.
- `pytest`, `ruff`, `mypy` pass with real output in the report; an independent review looks for a
  control that cannot fire, an invariant that passes vacuously, and any historical fact smuggled into
  a rule.
- `docs/phase-reports/V2-P05.md`, logical commits, clean tree, STOP. V2-P06 is not started.
