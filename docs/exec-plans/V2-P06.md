# V2-P06 — Calibration V2

Exec plan for `docs/phase-reports/V2-P06.md`, written before this phase's results were read. It
follows `docs/v2/phase-conventions.md` and implements `docs/OMP_UPGRADE_PLAN_V2.md` §6 (V2-P06).

## Why

V1 calibrated one parameter vector under one root seed and reported the result as a posterior. Two
things were wrong with that: a single weather sequence was treated as *the* run, and a single
population was treated as the posterior. The phase fixes both, and keeps the hold-out shut while it
does — a reserved window read during calibration cannot be read again afterwards.

## Scope

1. **Only evidence-backed, identifiable parameters.** Priors are exactly the parameter cards' ranges
   (V1's rule kept, not reinvented): a parameter has a prior exactly when its card carries a numeric
   range, and an `S`-grade parameter with no range is not in the table and cannot enter through a wide
   prior. The frozen artifact carries the prior, objective and evidence digests, so editing any of
   them changes the posterior's identity.
2. **Process seeds, not one weather sequence.** One draw is evaluated under a declared process seed
   set and its distance is the conditional distribution over them, not a single number. The
   **fixed-CRN** mode is kept as the declared contrast the phase asks for, and a one-seed set is
   refused as process uncertainty.
3. **A ladder with declared gates.** Rungs of particles × several sampler seeds, tempered stages per
   rung; whether to double is decided by the gates — location change against the prior range, the
   mechanism prediction, ESS per particle, acceptance, duplicate fraction — and a ladder that stops
   short is reported as non-converged rather than labelled.
4. **Every diagnostic in the manifest**: particles, sampler seed, stages, acceptance per stage, ESS
   and ESS per particle, duplicate fraction, simulation count, cache hits, wall-clock seconds and the
   failed-task list, beside the process and sampler seed sets and the gate bounds.
5. **A content-addressed freeze, and a hold-out that waits for it.** The posterior is hashed; the
   prediction path refuses to score a reserved window without that hash, and refuses a stale one too.
6. **Checkpoint and failure discipline**: a rung's artifact is written when it finishes, a resumed run
   reads it rather than re-simulating, and a failed evaluation is recorded and never merged as a
   particle.

## Non-goals

- No calibration of a parameter that has no card range, and no widening of a range to admit one.
- No tuning of a gate to make a ladder stop, and no particle count chased as a target.
- No hold-out or extrapolation score: the windows stay shut until a ladder converges and freezes.
- No V2-P07 work: the fixed-CRN contrast experiment and the sensitivity screen belong to that phase.

## Acceptance

- Process seeds, sampler seeds, particles, stages, acceptance, ESS, duplicate fraction and failed
  tasks all appear in the frozen artifact.
- The hold-out cannot be scored without the frozen hash, and window mixing fails closed in tests.
- The pilot is reproducible from the tree by one command, and its hash is produced by that command.
- `pytest`, `ruff`, `mypy` pass with real output in the report; an independent review looks for an
  unenforced gate, a vacuous diagnostic and a route past the freeze.
- `docs/phase-reports/V2-P06.md`, logical commits, clean tree, STOP. V2-P07 is not started.
