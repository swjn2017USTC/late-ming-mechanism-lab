# V2-P07 — Sensitivity & Counterfactual V2

Exec plan for `docs/phase-reports/V2-P07.md`, written before this phase's results were read. It
follows `docs/v2/phase-conventions.md` and implements `docs/OMP_UPGRADE_PLAN_V2.md` §6 (V2-P07).

## Why

V1 ran one Morris screen and one Sobol design of declared sizes and reported the indices; a reader
could not tell a stable ranking from a lucky one, or an adaptive choice from a pre-registered one.
The phase turns both into *ladders* over the same declared problem, records what each rung answered
and whether it moved, and reports non-convergence as a result rather than dressing a single rung as
an answer.

## Scope

1. **Morris ladder**: 20 trajectories, then a trajectory bootstrap of the top-k ranking, then 40 if
   the bootstrap disagrees with itself. Every level's indices, ranking and stability number are
   recorded. The screen covers **every declared prior** — the support the frozen posterior was drawn
   from — rather than a chosen corner of it.
2. **Sobol ladder**: N as powers of two, 64 → 128 → 256, `calc_second_order=False` first, over the
   screen's strongest parameters with the rest held at their cards' central values (named in the
   manifest). Second order runs only for the interaction candidates the model-comparison register
   pre-registered, and only after S1/ST have settled.
3. **PAWN** beside the Sobol indices for an output that is skewed or non-monotone, labelled a
   distributional statistic: agreement between two methods is not evidence of a mechanism.
4. **Paired CRN arms**: 16 → 32 → 64 replicates, stopping on the bootstrap interval's width and on
   the smallest effect the phase declared worth mentioning; absolute magnitude, paired difference,
   interval, direction stability and the minimum substantive effect reported together.
5. **Tipping**: a 5×5 coarse grid at 8 seeds, then adaptive points placed on the uncertain boundary
   with **new** seeds and re-verified on the seeds that were held back.
6. **Continuous outcomes, chain intermediates and the threshold ensemble**, and no quantitative
   reading for a near-constant output: it is reported as undefined with the reason.
7. **Every estimate as it came out**: NaN, negative, above one, with its interval. Nothing clipped.

## Non-goals

- No second-order Sobol outside the pre-registered interaction candidates.
- No ladder size chosen after seeing which size looked best, and no grid shown only where it worked.
- No reserve window scoring (V2-P06's freeze gate still governs) and no V2-P08 work.

## Acceptance

- Rankings, S1/ST, mechanism effects and boundaries stable after doubling; otherwise the phase
  reports non-convergence explicitly.
- Every adaptive choice and stop decision is in the manifest.
- A report's numbers are recomputable from `outputs/v2/p07/`, and a failed batch task is never
  interpolated into a result.
- `pytest`, `ruff`, `mypy` pass with real output in the report; an independent review looks for a
  gate that cannot fail, a clipped estimate and an unrecorded adaptive choice.
- `docs/phase-reports/V2-P07.md`, logical commits, clean tree, STOP. V2-P08 is not started.
