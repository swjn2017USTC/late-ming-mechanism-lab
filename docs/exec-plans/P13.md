# P13 - Mechanism Discovery / Mechanism Cards (scope)

Date: 2026-09-14. Depends on P08 (the evidence registry and its patterns), P09 (calibration and the
hold-out pass), P10 (ablations, sensitivity, counterfactuals, the tipping grid) and P12 (the same
world under three declared decision policies).

## What this phase does

Read everything those phases produced and write down what the model's behaviour actually supports as
a *mechanism*: six candidates, one card each, with the micro and meso conditions, the chain, the
trigger, the macro outcome, which conditions are necessary and which merely facilitate, the lag, and
then the evidence field by field - sensitivity, ablation, hold-out, policy robustness, historical
support and historical challenge, the counterexample, a falsifiable prediction and the uncertainty.

Each card carries a status from a five-word vocabulary and nothing else, and the status is argued
from the evidence rather than chosen:

```text
SUPPORTED      an intervention moved it and at least one other evidence kind agrees
CONDITIONAL    it holds under a declared condition and not outside it
WEAK           the evidence exists but leaves the claim unresolved
REJECTED       a measurement contradicts the claim as stated
UNIDENTIFIED   nothing in the project could have measured it
```

The five candidates the plan names are assessed, and a sixth is added because the evidence itself
named it: the model has no mortality mechanism at all, and a mechanism the project cannot measure
belongs in the record as `UNIDENTIFIED` rather than left out or blurred into a neighbour.

## Non-goals

- **No new simulation subsystem, no new mechanism, no parameter change.** P13 reads artifacts.
- No re-fitting and no re-running of the calibration; the ensemble is cited, not recomputed.
- No encoding of any historical outcome into a rule; the patterns stay comparison targets.
- No claim of causal historical truth. A card is a statement about this model under this design.
- No fifth-mechanism invention: the candidate set is the plan's list plus the one structural gap.

## Acceptance criteria

```text
docs/mechanisms/cards.yaml (machine-readable) · docs/mechanisms/M00N-*.md (human-readable) ·
docs/mechanisms/index.md · outputs/reports/mechanism-synthesis.md ·
every card with the brief's sixteen fields · statuses from the five-word vocabulary only ·
no vague multi-factor claims (the schema refuses them) ·
standalone risk-adjusted assessment · independent review · commit
```

## Deliverables

- `src/late_ming_lab/synthesis/schema.py` - the card schema, the vagueness refusal and the status
  rules (a `SUPPORTED` card must cite an intervention, not only quantities that were watched).
- `src/late_ming_lab/synthesis/evidence.py` - the bundle: the P09-P12 artifacts behind typed
  accessors, so a card cites a batch rather than a recollection.
- `src/late_ming_lab/synthesis/cards.py` - the six cards, every number read from the bundle.
- `src/late_ming_lab/synthesis/report.py` - the writers, including the fitted boundary section.
- `src/late_ming_lab/analysis/boundary.py` - the descriptive fit: logistic and boosted boundaries,
  partial dependence, tipping surfaces, with `CAUSAL_DISCLAIMER` rendered verbatim into the report.
- `docs/mechanisms/*` and `outputs/reports/mechanism-synthesis.md` - the products.
- `tests/unit/test_mechanism_schema.py`, `tests/unit/test_boundary.py`,
  `tests/integration/test_mechanism_synthesis.py`.
