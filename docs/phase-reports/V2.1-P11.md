# V2.1-P11 — Two Decisive Contrasts

Status: complete. Four trajectories, one per arm, `43.7 s` of simulation in total. Run against
`docs/exec-plans/V2.1-P11.md`; no V2.1-P12 work started.

## Entry condition, and what had to be built

V2.1-P10 established from the artifacts that the historical core is `deterministic-replay-by-design`,
so the entry condition holds and the design follows from it: **one trajectory per arm is a complete
sample**, because the run consumes no randomness and a second seed would replay the first. The
contrasts are therefore exact paired differences between two runs of the same code, input and window.

Neither contrast could be answered from an existing log, and both were refused rather than renamed:

- **M002.** V2-P03's `OPEN_MIGRATION_EXIT` arm is the *sandbox* baseline — toy dataset, synthetic
  climate, declared disruption and escalation regimes — so its configuration differs from the
  historical core's in the dataset, the forcing, the disruption regime and the extraction policy. The
  M002 card's own uncertainty says so: "the historical core's own gate contrast has not been run".
  P04's `migration-distress-route` opens the *destitution* route, which P04 itself describes as a
  bypass of eligibility and silver; the card's gate is `systems/migration.py#_permanent_gate`, whose
  leaf on this input is the household unmet-ratio line (`not-eligible` in 0.939 of cohort-months).
  A different gate is a different experiment.
- **M004.** P05's `elite-mediation-only` arm is *credit on, foreclosure off*; the card's falsifier
  names "the closed-credit arm", which no historical-core run had. The accumulating arm existed
  (P05's), and this phase re-runs it in the same batch so the paired difference is exact — and the
  digest cross-check below shows the re-run is P05's run.

## The four arms, and the two declarations they did not invent

| arm | what it changes | configuration diff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `reference` | nothing — the historical core at its neutral values | *(empty)* |
| `gate-open` | the declared `OPEN_MIGRATION_EXIT` opening of the mobility gate | `MigrationParameters.{cost_tael_per_household, cost_tael_per_adult, transit_loss_share, minimum_households_to_move}`, `HouseholdParameters.{permanent,temporary}_migration_unmet_ratio` |
| `elite-credit-closed` | the declared `NO_ELITE_CREDIT` ablation | `EliteParameters.loan_to_value` |
| `elite-accumulation` | P05's declaration, unchanged | `EliteParameters.{foreclosure_after_unserviced_months, foreclosure_land_share_of_pledge}` |

The two interventions are read from `experiments/interventions.py` — `open_migration_exit_updates()`
and `no_elite_credit_updates()`, extracted in this phase so the arm functions and the contrasts read
one declaration instead of two copies of the numbers. The gate has two leaves in this model (the
eligibility line and the road's cost) and the declared opening moves both; a caller that took half of
it would be running a different arm under the same name.

## Status recommendations, as the pre-registered rules decide them

| card | V2 status | recommended | why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **M002** | `CONDITIONAL` | **`CONDITIONAL`** (kept) | the paired difference is `+0.929244`, in the pre-registered direction and far above the protocol's `fiscal_base` minimum substantive effect of 0.02; the card's falsifier did not fire |
| **M004** | `WEAK` | **`WEAK`** (kept) | the branch fires (51 foreclosures, 13,063.8 mu transferred, 32,914.4 tael settled) and the difference is in the falsifier's direction, but `+0.008088` is **below** the declared 0.02: the branch's existence is measured and its effect is not |

What "0.02" is applied to, since the frozen protocol does not say it cleanly: `fiscal_base` declares
`unit: share of starting mu` and `minimum_substantive_effect: 0.02`, but `form: vector`, so the
outcome's *value* is its first measurement — `tax_base_change_mu`, in mu. The effect is applied to the
**relative change** the protocol's unit names, and the mu readings are reported beside it. That is the
reading the unit supports and the only one on which 0.02 is a number rather than a unit error; the
protocol file's own mismatch is recorded below rather than edited.

Three things this phrasing is careful about:

- "Stably differs", in the plan's rule, cannot mean across seeds here — there is one deterministic
  trajectory per arm and no seed variation to be stable over. Its pre-registered operationalization
  is the protocol's declared minimum substantive effect, which is what was applied; the
  recommendation quotes that number rather than a judgement.
- M004 does **not** rise, even though the direction the card predicts holds. The card says what a
  null means ("the card should say so in one sentence") and the plan says a rise needs the declared
  effect; `+0.008` is a quarter of it.
- Neither recommendation reads `breakdown`. M002 was kept while its line-6 share *fell* to zero, and
  M004's line-6 share is identical to the reference's; a rule that consulted the projection would
  have reached different conclusions from both.

### What the chains say about reading the gate contrast's size as an effect

Two measured caveats, printed in the document rather than left to a reader:

- The gate arm is the declared intervention **at its open values** — no move cost, no transit loss,
  both unmet-ratio lines at zero — so its cohorts move every month: `179,769` cumulative
  household-moves against the `50,400` households that started, and `23,349` out-of-region exits. The
  movers figure is a flow over 240 months and **not** a count of distinct households (the log carries
  60 cohort ids and 14,400 gate rows; 21,056 departure rows are emitted by those same 60 cohorts).
- An in-region move abandons land at the origin and the receiving cohort gains none:
  `receive_migrant_households` carries households, adults, grain, silver and assets, and no land. With
  cohorts moving every month, the base erodes further than the population that left would suggest —
  hence a 0.993 contraction against `23,349 / 50,400 = 0.46` of the starting households having left
  the region.

Neither reverses the contrast: the reference's base contracts by 0.064 with no gate opened at all. But
the *size* is a property of those two declarations, and the document says so where the numbers are.

## Carried to V2.1-P12

1. **M002's recommendation is conditional on an extreme declaration.** The gate contrast is `+0.93` on
   the base, produced by an arm whose declaration removes every cost and both eligibility lines. The
   card's uncertainty should carry the caveat list, not only the number.
2. **The land accounting asymmetry** — an in-region move abandons land at the origin and the
   destination gains none — is a property of the model that this contrast exposed. It is reported, not
   repaired: no source in the registry says what should happen to the abandoned mu, and repairing it
   would change every migration result in the project.
3. **P05's arms wrote parameter provenance for two sets only** (`holdout-arm.json` carried market and
   migration). That is fixed here for arms run from now on; the P05 and P04 directories keep what they
   wrote, and the fix is not retro-applied — a generated artifact is regenerated, not edited.
4. **M004 stays `WEAK` with its falsifier unanswered above the declared effect.** P12's card should say
   the branch exists, fires, points the right way, and is a quarter of the declared effect — which is
   what the card's own falsifier sentence asks for.
5. **`fiscal_base`'s unit and its measurement disagree.** The frozen protocol declares
   `unit: share of starting mu` with `form: vector` over `[tax_base_change_mu, tax_base_end_mu]`, so
   the value a caller receives is in mu while the unit, and the 0.02 effect declared beside it, are
   relative. Every phase reading this outcome has to make the same choice this one did; the protocol
   is frozen, so the choice is recorded here rather than fixed in the file.
