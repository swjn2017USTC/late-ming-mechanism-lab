### The translation is resolved by rule, not by fallback

`data/mechanisms/v2_1-zh.yaml` holds only the fields whose English *changed*: six summaries, six
sample notes and M002's and M004's uncertainty. Every other field is inherited from
`data/mechanisms/v2-zh.yaml`, and only when the English it translates is byte-identical to the V2
card's — a field whose English moved without a new translation is refused
(`test_a_changed_english_field_must_be_retranslated`), which is what stops a Chinese card rendering
English prose while looking translated.

### The handoff separates four things

`docs/v2_1/handoff.md` lists, under four headings: **reusable infrastructure** (the frozen protocol
and its isolation rule, the historical core and its forcing, the chain instrumentation, the typed
accessors and no-op detector, the replicate audit, the translation mechanism); **sealed negative
results** (M001's conditionality, M002's threshold-dependent half, M003's rejection, M004's sub-effect
branch, M006's refusal, P06/P07's non-convergence, P08's zero-decision pilot, the four classified
hold-out failures); **what only new data can change** (a mortality series, a converged posterior for
the reserved windows, data from outside the region, the registry decisions); and **what is not worth
more compute** (bigger ladders at the same evidence, a wider runtime corpus, national geography,
another reader pass over the published numbers, re-tuning the core to make a reserved window green).

## The bundle, in two commits

The bundle records `git_sha` and `tree_dirty` like V2's, with its own path excluded from the dirty
check. It is generated twice: once with the phase's other documents (naming the parent commit, tree
dirty — accurate about the moment of generation), and once more after the phase commit, on a clean
tree, so the committed bundle names the commit that carries the generating code. That is the same
sequence V2 used, and it is why the phase takes two commits rather than one.
