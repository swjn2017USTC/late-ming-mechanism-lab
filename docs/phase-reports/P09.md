# P09 — Calibration / Hold-out

Date: 2026-09-13 → 14
Status: complete. No P10 work started.

## Scope

Freeze the P08 registry, turn the target patterns into a declared objective, declare priors only
where a card already declares a range, run one small SMC batch, and report what the ensemble
identifies, what it trades off, what it cannot match, and how it behaves in the two thirds of the
window it was never fitted to. The scope document is `docs/exec-plans/P09.md`; this report is what
came out.

### Acceptance criteria

```text
windows declared once and enforced · objective proven blind to post-calibration events ·
every target pattern mapped to checks with a declared tolerance · no fitted magnitudes ·
priors bounded by P08 card ranges, refusal otherwise · pm.Simulator + sample_smc path ·
accepted-draw ensemble persisted with provenance and reproducible from its manifest ·
posterior predictive runs scored on hold-out and extrapolation · identifiable vs equifinal
verdict per parameter · patterns the model cannot match named · failures reported ·
P08 registry frozen by digest and checked · no mechanism changed to fit history
```

### Non-goals

No best-fit parameter set; no mechanism or parameter value changed to make a historical pattern come
out; no hold-out or extrapolation observation in any objective; no kernel, scheduler or system
change; no archival acquisition; no runtime LLM; no regional-scale compute.

## Contract decisions

1. **The objective is checks, not a distance to a number.** P08 recorded the target patterns as
   shapes ("dispersion rising as the crisis deepens", "receipts persistently below the quota"), not
   as series, so a historical least-squares distance does not exist for them. Each pattern becomes two
   or three checks whose thresholds come from the pattern's own wording — a sign, a ratio against the
   quota, a share above half — and the pattern's score is the share of its checks that failed. The
   sampler sees a Laplace kernel on that vector against zeros, with a declared tolerance of 0.25
   per pattern (about one failed check of the two or three each pattern carries).
2. **Nothing here is a fitted magnitude.** No check compares a model number with a recorded number.
   The one historical magnitude in the reports — the 1631 Shangzhou price, 40 tael per shi against a
   card band that stops at 2 — is printed in `mismatch.md` to be seen, with the consequence stated:
   a famine-price comparison can test direction and timing, never magnitude, until the mechanism is
   revisited on evidence.
3. **Only a card can bound a prior.** A parameter is calibratable when its card declares a numeric
   range, the field is a scalar the sandbox accepts, the card marks it a sensitivity priority, and at
   least one target check reaches its mechanism. That gives 13 of the 102 cards; 4 more carry a range
   and are excluded with the rule stated (`yield_shi_per_mu` and `rent_share_of_harvest` are
   dict-valued, so no single draw sets them; `land_per_adult_capacity_mu` and
   `wage_grain_shi_per_adult_month` are medium priority and unreached by any check); the remaining 85
   cards declare no range at all.
4. **The objective cannot read the future, structurally.** `Objective.score` refuses every window but
   the calibration window, and `PredictiveChecks.score` refuses the calibration window, so the two
   surfaces are disjoint by construction; a test asserts the disjointness both ways and another runs
   a full sandbox run, deletes every event after tick 119, and asserts the scores do not move.
5. **Common random numbers.** Every draw runs under the same root seed, so two draws that differ in
   one parameter share every RNG stream and a distance difference is attributable to the parameter.
   The simulator is deterministic; the test asserts two runs of one draw produce the same digest.
6. **An ensemble, not a fit.** The artifact is every particle with its parameter vector and its
   pattern scores. The identifiability verdict is the posterior interquartile range over the prior's
   full range (≤ 0.5 identified, ≥ 0.8 unresolved, between = weak); equifinality is the weighted
   correlation between draws (≥ 0.7 reported as a trade-off). Both thresholds are declared in
   `ensemble.py`, and every underlying number is persisted beside the verdict.
7. **The batch checks itself for collapse.** SMC resamples, and a particle cloud can collapse around
   a few ancestors while each marginal looks narrow. So the phase ran the same experiment twice,
   differing only in the sampler's seed, and compares locations as a share of the prior range: a
   parameter whose median moves by a quarter of its range is reported *unpinned* whatever its
   interquartile range said. This mattered: the first 12-particle batch the pipeline was validated on
   reported all 13 parameters identified with contractions of 0.03–0.44 and 34 trade-off pairs, which
   the 32-particle batches did not reproduce — the small batch was a collapse, and the stability rule
   is what caught it.
8. **The freeze is a digest, not a sentence.** `evidence_digest` hashes every registry file (sources,
   cards, ledger, rule claims, patterns), and the manifest records it with per-file digests. An edit
   to any of those files changes the batch's identity; a test copies the registry, edits one pattern
   file, and asserts the digest moves.

## The batch

```text
batch A  p09-cards87a14e9d-32p-ch1-s20260913   32 particles, 1 chain, seed 20260913
batch B  p09-cards87a14e9d-32p-ch1-s991753     32 particles, 1 chain, seed 991753
scenario toy fixture, synthetic climate shock (0.4 monthly probability, 0.6 severity floor),
nominal pressure 0.02, pay share 0.5, 300 troops per node, root seed 20260912, 240 ticks
batch A: 3 tempered stages, 288 sandbox runs, acceptance 0.41 / 0.40 / 0.43
batch B: 2 tempered stages, 160 sandbox runs, acceptance 0.50 / 0.52
frozen registry digest 87a14e9d12e69f3c over 24 files (10 pattern files, 9 card files, 2 source
registry files, 2 ledger files, 1 rule-claim file)
```

Cost on this machine: about 4–6 s per sandbox run, 288 runs per 32-particle batch — 20 to 45 minutes
depending on what else is running. The plan's instruction to validate the pipeline at small scale is
why the batches are 32 particles and the five-county fixture, not the regional one.

## What the ensemble says

**Identifiability (batch A).** Twelve of thirteen parameters contract to at most half their prior
range; `temporary_share_of_adults_per_month` is *weak* at 0.53. The narrowest is
`yield_loss_scale` (0.14), the widest identified `subsistence_grain_per_adult_month_shi` (0.46). That
sounds like a well-identified model, which is why the second batch matters.

**Seed stability.** Eleven of thirteen parameters hold their location across the two seeds. Two do
not: `interest_rate_monthly` moves 0.047 of its 0.09 range (shift 0.47 of the range) and
`permanent_migration_unmet_ratio` moves 0.255 of its range. Those two are reported unpinned — the
calibration window does not place them, and the phase says so instead of quoting a median.

**Equifinality.** At 32 particles no pair reaches the declared 0.7 correlation, so no trade-off is
reported for this ensemble. The 12-particle batch's 34 pairs were a resampling artifact, and the
report states the negative result rather than the earlier noise.

**Target patterns in the calibration window.** `land-abandonment-in-famine` and
`receipts-shortfall-chronic` are satisfied by every draw (all five checks), `debt-transfers-land` by
0.81 of them, `absorption-of-deserters-and-refugees` by 0.25. Two patterns are systematically
unsatisfied:

| pattern | failing check | satisfied share | what the failure is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `absorption-of-deserters-and-refugees` | `intake-tracks-desertion` | 0.25 | band in-take does not co-move with desertion at any draw |
| `famine-local-price-extremes` | `dispersion-tracks-distress` | 0.38 | the cross-node dispersion series is flat in most draws |
| `famine-local-price-extremes` | `dispersion-widens` | 0.44 | the worst year's dispersion is not above the first year's |

The dispersion failure needs reading carefully rather than as a mechanism verdict: across the
posterior draws the calibration window's cross-node price dispersion is zero in most runs (identical
posted prices, so the rank correlation is undefined and returns the no-variation sentinel), whereas
the default parameter set — which sits outside the prior support on `interest_rate_monthly` — does
produce a rising dispersion (0.003 → 0.55 across the window). So the statistic is measuring a regime
the sampler prefers for other reasons, and the pattern is neither refuted nor supported by it. That
is recorded here, in the mismatch document's medians, and as an open item, not patched by widening a
bound.

## What the ensemble fails at, out of sample

The eight patterns P08 reserved were scored after the ensemble was frozen. Over the whole run the
ensemble contradicts three of them, and in each case the failure is the pattern's own recorded
falsifier rather than a near miss:

| reserved pattern | check | satisfied share | the failure, in the pattern's own terms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `chongzhen-drought-sequence` | `shock-peak-in-late-years` | 0.00 | the worst shock year is 1625; the pattern's falsifier is "the model's worst simulated years falling in the warm-up period" |
| `famine-worst-years-1639-43` | `famine-events-concentrated-late` | 0.00 | 0.27 of the destitute cohort-months fall in the last five years — even spread, the falsifier the pattern names |
| `price-spike-concentration` | `price-spike-peak-late` | 0.44 | the worst dispersion year is 1625 in most draws; "the worst dispersion before the first severe shock" |

Four reserved patterns are not contradicted over the whole run: `many-bands-then-consolidation`
(0.94), `pay-monetised-and-arrears` (both checks 1.00), `quota-erosion-and-surcharge` (1.00), and
`shaanxi-net-outflow` (1.00 — with the qualification that exits are zero in the hold-out window and
only appear later, so the check passes on timing, and the volume of out-migration is tiny).

The first three failures are one finding, not three. The scenario's climate is
`SyntheticClimate(0.4, 0.6)` — an independent monthly draw with no trend — so no year is more likely
to be severe than another, and any pattern that says *the worst years were at the end* cannot come
out of it, whatever the mechanism parameters are. That is a property of a declared scenario
assumption, exactly the kind of thing the hold-out exists to expose, and the phase records it instead
of tuning the climate to pass its own check. The consequence for P10/P13 is concrete: either the
drought-timing claims cannot be tested in this model at all, or the climate layer needs a
reconstruction with a trend — a P08-sourced input, not a calibrated parameter.

Two failures are genuinely about the model rather than the scenario. `intake-tracks-desertion` fails
in three quarters of the draws: bands grow, and grow with distress, but not with desertion. And the
relief claim behaves differently by window — 0.84 of draws satisfy it over the whole run, 0.25 in the
hold-out — which is a caution about scoring a whole-run claim inside a sub-window rather than a
mechanism result.

## What this phase found (and fixed)

1. **A 12-particle batch that looked identified was collapsing.** All 13 parameters reported
   contractions of 0.03–0.44 and 34 trade-off pairs at |r| ≥ 0.7; the 32-particle batches show
   contractions of 0.14–0.53 and no pair above 0.7. SMC resampling had concentrated the particle
   cloud. The fix was not a wider prior but a second seed: `stability.md` is now part of the phase
   and reports two parameters as unpinned.
2. **A card records a value in use outside its own range.** `interest_rate_monthly` is declared
   `[0.01, 0.1]` while the model runs 0.005. The prior is the range, so the phase's own default run
   lies outside the calibration support. `centres_outside_range()` computes the finding from the
   registry, the objective report prints it, and a test pins the registry's current state so an edit
   to either side surfaces.
3. **Two cards carry a range for a field that is dict-valued.** `rent_share_of_harvest` declares
   `[0.3, 0.6]` but the field in use is a per-cohort-class mapping. It was moved from the calibrated
   set to the excluded list with that reason: a scalar draw would not be the value the model runs.
4. **The first objective draft cited a signature index the pattern does not have.**
   `land-abandonment-in-famine` carries one signature, and the base-contraction check pointed at a
   second. Building the objective refuses that, which is how it was caught before any sampling.
5. **The sampler's positional mapping is a real risk, and is now tested.** The Simulator RV hands
   PyMC's draws to the simulator positionally; a scrambled order would calibrate the wrong parameter
   and never look wrong. A test builds the model, calls the RV's function with known offsets and
   asserts each lands on the parameter whose position it was given.
6. **`parameter_hash` in a run manifest is still `None`.** The kernel does not know the parameter
   sets; the calibration manifest records them per draw instead, which is what P09 needs. Wiring the
   kernel's manifest to the parameter sets is left open rather than done in a calibration phase.

The invariants the suite enforces, in the words of the tests:

- the three split windows tile the run with no gap or overlap, and the whole-run view is not the
  calibration window;
- the objective refuses the hold-out, the extrapolation and the whole-run windows, and the prediction
  checks refuse the calibration window;
- the two check tables name disjoint pattern sets, and their union is exactly the registry's target
  and reserved patterns — a pattern added to either role breaks the build;
- the objective's score does not move when every event after tick 119 is deleted, on a real sandbox
  run;
- every yearly column group partitions the series contract, so no series column silently vanishes;
- the freeze digest is stable, changes when a registry file changes, and separates bounds from
  checks;
- a draw outside its prior range or missing a parameter is refused rather than clipped;
- the simulator is deterministic under common random numbers, and a changed parameter changes both
  the digest and the parameter hash;
- the ensemble round-trips through disk, and two batches are compared on location as a share of the
  prior range, with a seed-dependent shift reported as unpinned;
- the reports are regenerated from a batch directory and agree with the ensemble they describe.

## Open items for P10 and later

- **The climate scenario is stationary, so the drought-timing claims cannot be tested.** Three of the
  eight reserved patterns fail on this alone. Either a sourced climate reconstruction with a trend
  enters through P08, or those claims are declared untestable in this model.
- **The dispersion check is uninformative in the posterior region.** The cross-node price series is
  flat in most draws, so the two dispersion checks fail on no variation rather than on the wrong
  shape. The price rule's fixed target cover is the recorded suspect (`system-overview.md`), and a
  check that measures price *levels* against the card band would say more than a dispersion check
  does.
- **`interest_rate_monthly` and `permanent_migration_unmet_ratio` are unpinned** at 32 particles. A
  larger batch, a third seed, or a longer calibration window would be the way to settle them.
- **Two parameters sit at the edges of their own card bands** in the posterior
  (`subsistence_grain_per_adult_month_shi` at 0.35 of a 0.2–0.6 band, `food_shi_per_member_month` at
  0.37 of 0.2–0.5). Whether the band or the mechanism is wrong is an evidence question.
- **The exit check passes on timing, not on volume.** `households_exited` is zero inside the hold-out
  window and tiny over the run; the pattern's shape check cannot see that, and a volume-based
  statement would need the migration gate revisited (P07's open item).
- **32 particles is a pipeline, not a posterior.** The identifiability table should be read as
  "which parameters move together under this objective", not as a calibrated parameter set.
- **No comparison with the medium fixture.** The phase ran the toy space only; whether the same
  checks and the same verdicts hold at twelve counties is untested.
- **`parameter_hash` remains unwired in `RunManifest`** (see above).
