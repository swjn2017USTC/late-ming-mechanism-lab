## Scope

Give V2 an evidence base whose inputs are located, legal and honest about how far they were read: a
snapshot manifest schema with rights and hashes, the public sources V2 will actually use opened and
checked, the thirteen records P08 left unverified worked through, the acquisition queue made
machine-readable, the evidence tasks ranked, and the reports taught to carry rights, verification
and read depth. The scope document is `docs/exec-plans/V2-P01.md`; this report is what came out.

## Contract decisions

1. **A snapshot is a file with a licence, and the licence is read, not assumed.** Every record
   carries `rights.status`, `redistribution`, the statement itself, the document it was read from,
   and the date. An `unknown` licence must state the question that would settle it, so "not checked"
   cannot read as "no restriction".
2. **A locator is a citation; a `fetch_url` is what a tool fetches.** The review pointed out that
   seven of eleven records carried a DOI landing page as their locator and the real file endpoint
   only inside a prose field, so re-acquisition would have failed for most of the manifest. Every
   http record now carries `fetch_url`, the script uses it, and the validator refuses an http record
   without one.
3. **Raw files never enter git.** Every `local_path` is under `data/raw/private/`, which
   `.gitignore` excludes; the validators refuse anything else, an invariant test asks git what is
   tracked, and the acquisition script refuses to write outside it. What the repository keeps is the
   record, the rule and the hash.
4. **`verified` is not `read`.** `verification` says a bibliographic identity was confirmed against a
   page that loaded; the new `read_depth` says how much of the text was read. An unverified record
   may not claim to have read its own content, and must carry a `next_action`, because the
   acquisition queue is now data rather than a paragraph.
5. **Grades move only with the evidence.** No card or pattern grade changed and no parameter value
   was touched. Four ledger grades changed: `gao-2024-shaanxi-famine` was corrected and re-graded
   D→C in the registry, and three grade-A claims were downgraded to C because their own notes said
   the figures came from search results. Each change is annotated in the record it changed.
6. **The ranking is arithmetic over the registries.** `score = relevance × debt`, with debt =
   grade-S/D cards + unread sources + pending inputs (+2 when no mechanism exists). Relevance is the
   only authored input; the components are printed so a reader can re-rank with their own weights.
7. **Rights enter the reports, not a side note.** `coverage.md` carries access, licence and
   redistribution per source and per snapshot, plus what was read; `gaps.md` carries the open licence
   questions and the acquisition queue with its next steps; `uncertainty.md` names, for each card,
   every source behind it with its verification and read depth.

Version `1` also matters: CHGIS V6's data dictionary is a V4 artifact shipped with V6, and the
REACHES construction paper documents the digitization of the Qing volume (1644–1795) while the file
spans 1368–1911. Both are recorded in the snapshot notes because they bound what the files can
support.

## What this changed in the evidence base

- **Sources: 25 → 28; verified: 12 → 21; unverified: 13 → 7.** Read depth across the 28: **0
  full-text, 6 abstract, 22 identity-only.** Nothing in this registry has had its content fully read;
  the two `full-text` marks the first draft carried were on *dataset* sources whose licence and
  readme had been read, which is not the same thing, and the review caught it. What was read of each
  file is recorded per snapshot instead.
- **Twelve of the twenty-nine ledger entries carry grade A or B while every source behind them is
  identity-only**, down from fifteen because the review found three grade-A entries whose own notes
  say the figures were read in search results rather than in the sources. Those three
  (`mil-finance-treasury-share`, `mil-finance-frontier-grain`, `mil-finance-grain-to-silver`) were
  downgraded to C with the reason recorded in the entry; the project's own definition of grade A
  requires the source to record the quantity itself. Reading the named pages would restore a higher
  grade. The remaining twelve are B judgements about source quality, disclosed by name in the
  coverage report.
- **The P09 calibration batch is now identified by a registry that no longer exists.** Its frozen
  `evidence_digest` is `87a14e9d…`; the registry as of this phase digests to `83b40966…`. That is the
  intended consequence of editing the registry, and it is exactly why the V1 freeze manifest exists:
  the batch remains reproducible from its own recorded digest, and no V1 artifact was touched.
- **The evidence tasks are ranked** (top to bottom): military pay and desertion 150, governance
  thresholds 120, migration 96, climate 95, relief 88, debt and land 87, prices 52, mortality 35.
  Mortality ranks last by construction — it has no cards to be weak — so the report prints its
  "no mechanism" line with the score rather than letting a low number read as "not important".

What the tests defend, in the words of the tests:

- an acquired snapshot without a hash, size, timestamp or path is refused; a pending one must say
  what it is waiting for; a human-only acquisition can never be recorded as acquired;
- a `local_path` outside `data/raw/private/` is refused, and no raw file is tracked by git;
- an unknown licence without a question, and a manifest with a duplicate id or a stale schema
  version, are refused;

  ordered by score;

## Independent review

An independent reviewer agent (`V2P01Review`, read-only) re-opened locators itself, re-hashed snapshot
files, recomputed the ranking by hand from the registries, and checked every rights judgement against
its source. Verdict: **approve with follow-ups**, no blockers. Its findings and the dispositions:

| # | severity | finding | disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **MAJOR** | `read_depth: full-text` on `chgis-v6` and `reaches-noaa` overstated what was read: the *licence* and the *readme* were read in full, not the datasets — and the coverage report and the task ranking inherited the error | Fixed: both records are `identity-only` with the reason in the record, and the registry file now states the convention ("a licence, a readme or a dictionary read in full does not make the dataset read"). Read depth is now 0 full-text / 6 abstract / 22 identity-only, and the ranking moved (governance 115→120, climate 90→95, migration 92→96, mortality 30→35) with no rank changing |
| 2 | **MAJOR** | Three grade-A ledger entries (`mil-finance-treasury-share`, `mil-finance-frontier-grain`, `mil-finance-grain-to-silver`) are inflated by the project's own definition of A — their notes say the figures were read in search results and the pages never opened — and the phase had disclosed them without acting | Fixed: all three downgraded to C with the reason recorded in the entry. The reviewer's own reading is that the other twelve A/B-on-identity-only entries are defensible source-quality judgements; they stay, named in the coverage report |
| 3 | MINOR | `fetch_snapshots.py` could re-acquire only 4 of 11 records: seven carried a DOI landing page as their locator and the real file endpoint only in a prose field, so a re-fetch would raise or download the wrong bytes | Fixed: `fetch_url` added to the acquisition record, required for every http snapshot and used by the script; the validator refuses an http record without one |
| 4 | MINOR | The access table shows `human-only: 0` while four sources are institution-gated | No change: the counts are per access condition, and the four gated sources are listed by name immediately below the table |

The reviewer verified as holding: that none of the nine newly verified records was marked verified on
a search-result title (it re-opened `gao-2024`, `brook-2017`, `brook-2023`, `chgis-v6`, `reaches-noaa`
and `wang-2018` itself, and confirmed `zheng-2014` through Crossref); the grade caps and the
`next_action` presence on all seven unverified records; all four rights judgements as "the right way
round and none more permissive than the source", including a rule-by-rule audit of the twelve
`derived_output_rule`s; that `git ls-files data/raw` is empty, that four snapshot files re-hash to
the manifest, and that nothing under `outputs/` changed; and the ranking arithmetic (two domains
recomputed from the registries by hand). It could not verify the full suite (out of its scope) or
the page content behind the two identity-only records that carry no figures, and says so.

Two of its findings are worth stating as method rather than as fixes: the read-depth field only
means something if it describes *the source* and not the documents beside it, and a phase that
publishes a list of over-claimed grades without touching them has disclosed a problem rather than
corrected one.
