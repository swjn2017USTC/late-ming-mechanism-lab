# P05 — Fiscal Extraction / Governance / Relief

Date: 2026-09-12
Status: complete. No P06 work started.

## Scope

Give the model a county fiscal apparatus: assessment against a tax base, a collection effort with
a cost, receipts, arrears, official relief funded from the treasury, elite tax mediation driven by
a real demand, and an extraction policy. Then run the pressure sweep the prompt asks for and
report the measurements — without claiming the mechanism the sweep might be used to test.

### Acceptance criteria

```text
CountyGovernment · state capacity split into TaxCollectionCapacity, InformationCapacity,
ReliefCapacity, CoercionCapacity, LogisticsCapacity with no scalar · tax quota · collection
effort · collection cost · actual receipts · tax arrears · tax base · official relief · relief
inventory and fiscal cost · elite tax mediation · extraction escalation policy · an experiment
that raises extraction pressure and observes nominal quota, actual receipt, tax base, migration
eligibility and asset depletion · no claim that Fiscal Extraction Inversion exists · tests for
fiscal accounting, tax sources, no double collection and relief conservation
```

### Non-goals (explicitly not built)

No military finance, armies, desertion or armed groups; no actual migration; no land-tax reform,
no corvée or labour levy, no central government; no calibration; no LLM. Arrears have no
consequence beyond being owed (see the open items: that is a declared gap, not a design).

## Contract decisions

1. **Five capacities, never one number.** `StateCapacity` has exactly five fields and no
   aggregate; `hidden_land_share` is the only place two capacities meet, and it is a named rule
   (`elite_hidden_share × (1 − information)`), not a summary index. Tests assert that no mean,
   index, total or `state_capacity` attribute exists, and each capacity has its own measured role.
2. **The tax ledger is decomposed.** Quota, effort, cost, receipts and arrears are separate
   quantities, each logged, each in the monthly county record, and reconciled: everything pursued
   is either received or owed, and net receipts are receipts minus the cost of collecting them.
3. **The base is what the county can see.** Household land is assessed; elite land escapes in
   proportion to weak information. Land that moves to elites therefore leaves the base, which is
   the mechanism an inversion hypothesis would need — and the experiment measures how little of it
   actually happens here.
4. **Collection reuses the household's own machinery.** Mediation, silver, forced grain sale,
   movable goods, land and loans — no new institutions, and every step logged on both sides. A
   household keeps half a year of its own need before selling grain to pay tax.
5. **Official relief is funded, not conjured.** The county buys grain at the posted price and
   releases it under `ReliefCapacity`; the fiscal cost is the purchase plus logistics, and the
   granary never goes negative.
6. **Elite mediation now has a demand.** The assessed obligation is what an elite may advance;
   the P04 placeholder knob (`tax_demand_tael_per_household`) is deleted rather than left beside
   the real path, and the mediation block moved from the elite system to the tax system.
7. **Two policy answers, kept separate.** A policy sets both the assessment rate and the
   collection effort; `ArrearsEscalation` raises both as the arrears *ratio* grows, bounded by the
   declared effort ceiling and floored by coercion capacity. The ratio's denominator is what the
   base would bear at the nominal rate, so it has a stationary meaning; `FixedExtraction` ignores
   the arrears it creates, which is what makes the comparison meaningful.
8. **No verdict column.** The experiment's output columns are declared and tested; the phase
   supplies data and refuses the hypothesis.
9. **The P03 and P04 configurations stay reproducible.** The fiscal layer is on by default in the
   assembly, but the P03 and P04 experiments pin `with_fiscal=False`, so their published answers
   remain answers to their own configurations. Re-measuring those four questions under the fiscal
   layer is a later phase's job, and the flag says so in the code.
10. **Arrears are a claim with no teeth yet.** Households carry `tax_arrears_tael` as a distinct
   balance from elite debt, and the county's stock is the sum of theirs. Nothing is seized for
   arrears, no interest accrues on them, and no coping step is triggered by them. This is stated
   wherever the sweep is reported, because it is what makes the migration result an artefact
   rather than a finding.

## The extraction-pressure experiment

Full 1625–1644 window, toy economy, severe forcing (0.5 / 0.6) throughout. The pressure is the
assessment rate against the visible land base; two policy families are swept.

Fixed-effort policy (effort 0.5):

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.005 | 74,191 | 20,396 | 0.275 | 9,280 | 0.44 | 180,750 → 175,655 | 15,029 | 0.52 |
| 0.01 | 148,072 | 36,522 | 0.247 | 22,707 | 1.11 | 180,750 → 175,192 | 15,168 | 0.52 |
| 0.02 | 296,102 | 64,489 | 0.218 | 53,952 | 2.97 | 180,750 → 175,228 | 15,157 | 0.48 |
| 0.04 | 593,650 | 115,609 | 0.195 | 121,851 | 7.42 | 180,750 → 175,899 | 14,955 | 0.40 |
| 0.08 | 1,184,327 | 194,081 | 0.164 | 279,649 | 17.13 | 180,750 → 175,489 | 15,078 | 0.44 |
| 0.16 | 2,360,336 | 273,923 | 0.116 | 670,212 | 42.58 | 180,750 → 174,803 | 15,284 | 0.36 |

Arrears-escalating policy, same nominal pressures: the assessment rate rises from 1.0× to 1.5×
nominal as the arrears ratio grows, effort rises 0.72 → 0.91, receipts/quota falls 0.286 → 0.077,
and arrears reach 2.81M tael at the top of the range. The rate and the effort are now genuinely
proportional to the arrears the policy reads (see the review note below: the first wiring made
every escalating policy a step function).

Asset depletion over the sweep: movable goods sold to pay tax rises 254 → 907 tael in total;
grain sold to pay tax is essentially zero (0.13 → 0.49 shi across the whole run, because a
household keeps half a year of its own need before selling); and **land sold to pay tax is zero at
every pressure**, because the only buyer — the local elite — has lent its silver away (its cash
box is empty at the end of the high-pressure run) and cannot purchase.

Other measured traces: relief released rises 8,374 → 76,909 shi and then plateaus; the mean unmet
share *falls* slightly, 0.250 → 0.232; and migration eligibility *falls*, 0.52 → 0.36.

### What this does not say

**P05 does not claim that Fiscal Extraction Inversion exists.** The measurements above are data for
testing it, and the honest reading of the data is that the mechanism is *not* established here:

- receipts rise across the whole range instead of collapsing; what falls is the share of the
  quota collected;
- the base barely erodes (visible land falls 3% over twenty years, and none of that fall is
  tax-driven), so the base-erosion channel is essentially closed in this configuration;
- arrears accumulate to 43 tael per household with no consequence whatsoever, so the households
  that cannot pay are not made worse off by failing to pay;
- the migration indicator moves *against* the folk expectation, because higher receipts fund more
  official relief — a coupling of extraction and relief that the current parameters produce.

Three things would have to change before anyone may claim the inversion: arrears must bite (seizure,
interest or exclusion from relief), the base must be able to move out of reach (a funded land
buyer, or a hiding rule that responds to pressure), and the relief channel must be separable from
the extraction channel so the two effects can be told apart. All three are open items below.

The four required test categories, all run against a real extraction run:

- **fiscal accounting** — every county-month reconciles: net receipts equal receipts minus the
  cost of collecting them; receipts never exceed what was pursued; pursuit never exceeds the
  assessed quota; every pursued tael is either received or recorded as arrears.
- **tax sources** — county silver enters only through `TAX_RECEIPT` and leaves only through
  collection cost, relief logistics or grain purchase; granary grain enters only by purchase and
  leaves only as relief; every receipt has a household payment or an elite advance behind it, and
  the two sides match exactly.
- **no double collection** — a household's payments in one tick never exceed its share of what was
  pursued, and never take more than the declared number of collection steps; the duplicate-channel
  check was replaced during the phase by that bound, because paying in instalments through the same
  liquidation channel is normal and the first version of the test was wrong.
- **relief conservation** — official relief released equals relief received (`:official` channel
  only, so private relief is not mixed in); the granary reconciles from its opening balance through
  every purchase and release and never goes negative; county arrears equal the arrears its
  households owe, to the tael.

## Open items for P06 and later

- **Arrears have no teeth.** No seizure, no interest, no exclusion from relief, no distress. Until
  one of those exists, any statement about extraction pressure and household behaviour is about a
  claim nobody enforces — and the migration result above is the clearest example.
- **No funded land buyer at high pressure.** The elite's silver is exhausted by mediation and
  lending before it can buy land, so the tax-driven land-transfer channel never opens. Whether that
  is realistic or an artefact of the order in which elites spend is a question for P06/P09.
- **The base cannot respond to pressure.** Land escapes only through elite accumulation and only
  at the rate information capacity allows; there is no concealment response, no registration
  collapse and no abandonment of land, which are the classic erosion channels.
- **Relief and extraction are coupled through the treasury.** Higher receipts buy more grain and
  release more relief, which is one reason the distress indicators move the "wrong" way. P10's
  ablations (`HIGH_RELIEF`, `NO_EXTRACTION_ESCALATION`) are the right instrument to separate them.
- **One land tax, one rate.** No corvée, no salt or commercial levies, no surcharges, no
  apportionment between counties, and no arrears recovery round.
- **Extraction policy is a rule, not a decision.** The institutional layer of P11 will choose
  among bounded actions from an observed state; today's policies read arrears and nothing else.
