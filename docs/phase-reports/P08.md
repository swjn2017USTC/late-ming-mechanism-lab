# P08 — Historical Evidence / Parameter Registry

Date: 2026-09-13
Status: complete. No P09 work started.

## Scope

Build the chain from simulation claim to evidence to parameter and rule provenance, and make it
checkable: a source registry, an evidence ledger, parameter cards for every parameter the model
runs, a rule inventory that relates every rule to evidence-backed, theoretically-assumed or
exploratory, and a historical pattern registry for pattern-oriented comparison later. Then report
what the evidence base covers, what it leaves uncertain, and where the gaps are.

This is an evidence phase: it does not tune a parameter, does not change a mechanism to fit a
historical outcome, and records no number that a source did not state.

### Acceptance criteria

```text
sources/registry · evidence ledger · parameter cards · historical pattern registry ·
grades A/B/C/D/S · ten evidence clusters (drought/climate, famine, agriculture,
population/migration, grain market/prices, land/debt/elites, taxation/levies, relief,
military finance, rebellions/armed groups) · every rule related to evidence-backed /
theoretically-assumed / exploratory · historical_patterns/*.yaml · evidence coverage report ·
parameter uncertainty report · evidence gaps · reuse classified COPY/CONFIGURE/REBUILD/DO NOT COPY ·
no parameter tuning · no mechanism changed to fit history · no invented numbers
```

### Non-goals

No calibration or fitting of any kind; no mechanism edits; no acquisition of licensed material by
an agent; no porting of the sibling project's sources, excerpts or conclusions; no sourcing of the
archival series (county quota books, relief registers, garrison payrolls) that the phase names as
gaps.

## Contract decisions

1. **The card is the gate.** `docs/epistemics/evidence-grades.md` says a parameter without a card
   does not enter the model, and that is now enforced against the live parameter models rather than
   a hand-kept list: the invariant suite walks every field of every parameter set and fails if a
   card is missing. Two further checks keep the cards honest — a card's recorded value must equal
   the value the model actually runs, and a card may not document a parameter that no longer
   exists.
2. **Support class is not grade.** Every card declares `evidence-backed`, `theoretically-assumed` or
   `exploratory` *separately* from its A–D/S grade, because they answer different questions: the
   grade is how good the evidence is, the class is what kind of claim the value is. The phase's
   distribution: **11 rules evidence-backed, 7 theoretically assumed, 6 exploratory; 9 of 102
   parameters carry a source for their value and 23 more are framed by a claim** without one
   fixing the value.
3. **A grade asserts what was checked.** `verified` means the bibliographic identity was confirmed
   against a page that was loaded; `unverified` means it was recorded from memory, a search result
   or a secondary citation. The schema *rejects* an unverified record graded A or B, so the doubt
   cannot be washed out. Result: 12 of 25 sources verified, 13 unverified and capped at C or D.
4. **Layers stay separate.** 3 primary, 4 dataset, 15 modern scholarship, 3 reference works, counted
   apart in the coverage report at every step. A claim resting on a gazetteer, a reconstruction and
   a monograph is recorded with the layer named.
5. **Restricted access is structural.** `access: institution` or `human-only` requires a
   `rights_note`; the registry has no field for a local path to licensed material, and an invariant
   asserts that no locator is a path inside this repository. Acquisition is a human act, and for
   licensed material it does not happen here.
6. **Reference-price honesty.** Where a source documents a famine extreme the model's ceilings
   exclude, the card says so. The 1631 Shangzhou price and the Yansui series (a twentyfold rise to
   4 tael per shi) are recorded with a note that the model's price ceiling understates the recorded
   worst years. That is a known limitation, not a calibration.
7. **Two claims per cluster minimum, patterns with signatures.** Every cluster carries sources,
   claims and at least one pattern. Every pattern names the quantity the model already reports, the
   shape the record shows, and how a later phase could score it *without* fitting, plus the
   observations that would count against the model.
8. **Hold-outs are marked now.** Eight patterns are `hold-out`, five `target`, seven `constraint`,
   two `not-calibrated`. A later phase inherits the decision rather than choosing it after seeing
   its own results.

| famine | 7 (2 primary, 1 dataset, 4 modern) | 4 | 5 | 3 |
| agriculture | 3 (1 dataset, 2 modern) | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| population_migration | 2 (1 modern, 1 reference) | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| grain_market_prices | 5 (2 primary, 3 modern) | 3 | 4 | 2 |
| land_debt_elites | 6 (2 primary, 1 dataset, 2 modern, 1 reference) | 3 | 5 | 2 |
| taxation_levies | 7 (2 primary, 1 dataset, 3 modern, 1 reference) | 3 | 1 | 2 |
| relief | 5 (2 primary, 2 modern, 1 reference) | 3 | 5 | 2 |
| military_finance | 5 (1 primary, 4 modern) | 3 | 4 | 2 |
| rebellion_armed_groups | 6 (2 primary, 2 modern, 2 reference) | 3 | 5 | 2 |

What the strongest claims actually support, and what they do not:

- **Drought and climate (B).** Documentary reconstructions agree on a multi-year North China
  drought with the worst phase in the late 1630s and early 1640s; onset is disputed by one to two
  years between sources. No source gives a precipitation figure the model can consume, so the
  mapping from anomaly to lost yield remains the model's.
- **Famine (B).** The sequence (deficit → harvest failure → famine) and the concentration of the
  worst years in 1639–1643 are documented; the 1631 Shangzhou price of 4 tael per dou is a real
  local observation. Nothing measures unmet need or mortality at county resolution.
- **Agriculture (C).** Northern dryland yields are reconstructed in a 0.2–1.0 shi per mu band with
  a weighted average near 0.77–0.8; the model's per-zone values sit inside that band. This is the
  closest any parameter gets to a sourced value, and it is still one inference away.
- **Prices (C–B).** A twentyfold long-run rise to the 1631 peak, and a concentration of famine-price
  observations in 1639–1643: direction and timing, not a series.
- **Taxation and relief (B, form).** The quota structure, its erosion, the single-whip
  consolidation, the layered granary institutions and the multiple relief channels are all
  documented in form; no source in this registry quantifies a county's receipts or a relief
  coverage rate.
- **Military finance (A).** The strongest evidence in the phase: Ray Huang's figures on the
  military share of central treasury silver (53–85 per cent, occasionally higher) and on frontier
  dependence on transported grain. The model's county-level share is a different quantity at a
  different level of government, which the card states.
- **Armed groups (B).** Thirty-plus bands in the early 1630s, a field army of 60,000–100,000 by
  1644 with scholarly disagreement recorded, and growth by absorbing bands, surrendered soldiers
  and refugees. The model's bands are of a different order and the comparison is about shape.

The invariant suite enforces, in both directions, the properties the phase is about:

- every parameter the model runs has a card, no card documents a parameter that does not exist, and
  every card's recorded value equals the value in use;
- every source cited by a card, a claim or a pattern is registered; every claim a card cites exists;
  every parameter a claim supports has a card; **every rule a claim names is a declared rule** —
  the check that caught two agents' claims pointing at rule names the code does not have;
- an unverified source never carries grade A or B, and grade S never cites a source;
- no claim, pattern or card is graded above its best source, and no unverified source carries a
  claim graded above it;
- at least one pattern is held out of any future fit, and every pattern declares a role and an
  observable signature;
- both layers are present in the registry, restricted sources state their condition, and no locator
  is a path inside this repository.

The report tests regenerate all three documents into a temporary directory and check their numbers
against the registries, so a report cannot drift from the data it summarises.

## What this phase found (and fixed)

1. **Eighteen citations let an unverified source carry a claim stronger than itself.** The audit
   found records whose identity was confirmed but whose pages were never opened being listed as
   support for claims graded above them, which would have read as corroboration between sources when
   part of the list was never read. The citations were removed, each claim's remaining sources carry
   it, and the property is now a test.
2. **Two agents' claims cited rules that do not exist.** The drought and famine claims pointed at
   `yield-loss`, `grain-price-spike`, `famine-onset` and similar names, while the code declares
   `agricultural-state`, `market-clearing` and `coping-ladder`. The reverse-direction check was
   added because of it; without it the chain would have looked complete while being broken.
3. **One claim had no rule to attach to.** The population claim referred to famine mortality, and
   the model has no mortality mechanism — population changes only through migration and
   recruitment. That is recorded as a structural gap rather than attached to an unrelated rule.
4. **A grade/verification conflict.** A source recorded as unverified was first graded B; the
   schema now rejects that combination, and the record was capped at C with the reason recorded.
5. **The price ceiling understates the record.** The model's ceiling excludes prices of the order
   the gazetteers record in 1631. Recorded on the card and in the price pattern rather than
   corrected, because correcting it would be tuning the mechanism to a historical outcome.
6. **Seasonal migration is gated on distress, and the record says it was routine.** The pattern
   records the model's behaviour as a known limitation with a falsifier, instead of adjusting the
   eligibility rule to produce it.

## Review: what was possible, and what replaced it

An independent agent review was attempted twice and **could not run**: the development-time provider
returned `401 Insufficient balance` for both `opencode-go/glm-5.3-flash` (the configured reviewer
model) and `opencode-go/deepseek-v4.1-flash`. The runtime USTC model was **not** substituted, because
the project's boundary keeps it for the P11 institutional-decision layer and out of development
work (`.omp/RULES.md` rules 8–12); using it here would have crossed the separation this phase is
supposed to document. The limitation is recorded rather than papered over, and the strongest
substitute available was run instead: a mechanical adversarial audit of exactly the properties a
reviewer would attack, with its findings fixed and then *enforced as tests*.

The audit, and what it found:

| check | result | action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is any claim, pattern or card graded above its **best** source? | none — no grade inflation | property now enforced by `test_no_claim_pattern_or_card_is_graded_above_its_best_source` |
| Does any claim cite an **unverified** source at a grade above what was checked? | yes, 18 citations across 9 claims and 7 patterns | citations trimmed; the remaining sources carry each claim; property enforced by `test_no_unverified_source_carries_a_claim_graded_above_it` |
| Is a source recorded as never opened cited as *support*? | once (`gao-2024-shaanxi-famine`, an existence-only stub, cited for a B claim) | citation removed and the reason recorded in the claim's notes |
| Do all four registries resolve in both directions? | clean | seven referential tests, including claims→rules, which had already caught two agents' non-canonical rule names |
| Is any locator a path to acquired material, or a restricted source without a condition? | clean | enforced structurally by the schema and by `test_human_only_acquisition_is_recorded_not_bypassed` |
| Do the reports agree with the registries they summarise? | clean | `tests/integration/test_evidence_reports.py` regenerates all three and checks the numbers |

Two caveats on that table, stated because they limit what it proves. First, the audit was mechanical
and written by the same author as the data: it can prove that no grade exceeds its sources and that
every reference resolves, but it cannot independently judge whether a citation is *apt* — whether a
source about a border market supports a claim about a province, for instance. Second, the two agents
who compiled the sources were read-only scouts working from open web sources; their verification
notes are recorded per source, and 13 of 25 records were never opened. A human reviewer with library
access is still the right instrument for the aptness question, and the acquisition queue at the end
of `docs/evidence/gaps.md` is where that work starts.

## Open items for P09 and later

- **The registry holds no archival material.** County quota books, relief registers, garrison
  payrolls and price series at county resolution are the sources that would constrain the model's
  central parameters, and they are named as gaps rather than approximated.
- **Nothing measures the model's own central quantities at the model's resolution.** No claim in
  the registry measures monthly unmet need, a migration rate, a desertion rate, a band's extraction,
  or a county's receipts; those parameters stay grade S, 70 of them with no claim attached at all.
- **Thirteen sources were recorded but not opened.** Each states what could not be confirmed and
  the query that was run; they cap the grade of every claim resting on them.
- **The model's price ceiling excludes the recorded famine extremes**, so a famine-price comparison
  can test direction and timing but not magnitude until the mechanism is revisited on evidence —
  which is a P09/P10 decision, not a P08 edit.
- **Modern scholarship outnumbers primary material in the registry** (15 to 3). That is a statement
  about what could be verified openly in this phase, and it is why so many cards are framed
  (`form`, `direction`) rather than fixed (`magnitude`).
- **Four sources are behind institutional access** and sit in the acquisition queue as human work:
  the Ming Veritable Records, Guo Songyi on yields, Quan Hansheng on northern prices, and the local
  gazetteer collection.
- **No pattern has been scored.** The registry says what could be compared and what would falsify
  the model; P09 decides the comparison, and the eight hold-outs are already reserved.

## Changes to earlier phases

- No mechanism, parameter value or rule changed. The phase adds an evidence layer beside the model;
  the only code touched outside `evidence/` is the report entry point and two analysis modules that
  already read the log.
- `docs/epistemics/evidence-grades.md` gained the implemented file layout and the
  COPY/CONFIGURE/REBUILD/DO NOT COPY classification of what was reused from the earlier Academic
  Literature Pipeline: its schema and workflow were adapted, its content was not read or copied,
  and the human acquisition gate is now enforced by the registry schema rather than by convention.
