# P07 — Integrated Crisis Engine

Date: 2026-09-13
Status: complete. No P08 work started.

## Scope

Connect P02–P06 into one sandbox and make the last link real. Migration was the missing mechanism —
P03 decided who *could* leave and moved nobody — and without it the model had armies and bands
competing for labour with a population that could not walk away from either. This phase adds
migration, an explicit scheduler with a derived dependency diagram, a twelve-county fixture, the
toy and medium 240-tick runs, the metric set the phase asks for, and a fixed-seed regression suite
that pins the whole sandbox.

### Acceptance criteria

```text
monthly scheduler · explicit step order · system dependency diagram (derived from the code) ·
integrated event trace · toy 5-county scenario · medium 12-county scenario · 240-tick runs ·
fixed-seed regression suite · real migration (permanent, temporary, exits) ·
metrics: household distress distribution · land concentration · migration · grain price dispersion ·
actual tax receipts · tax base · military arrears · desertion · number of armed groups ·
largest band share · governance failure indicators · an independent review
```

### Non-goals (explicitly not built)

No new mechanism beyond migration; no calibration, no historical parameter fitting, and no claim
that any of the reported surface describes the Ming collapse. No inter-county military movement, no
trade disruption driven by band activity, no land market response to migration, no births or deaths,
no central government, no LLM. Every fixture value remains a grade `S` assumption.

## Contract decisions

1. **The scheduler validates dependencies, not just order.** Each system declares which shared
   resources it reads and writes; the scheduler refuses a registration where a system reads a
   resource before the earliest phase that writes it. The diagram in
   `docs/architecture/system-dependency.md` is generated by `core.scheduler.as_mermaid` from those
   declarations, so it cannot drift: it is a phase-level dependency claim, and the doc says so.
2. **The wiring is part of the replay key.** `RunManifest` now carries `systems` and
   `systems_digest` as required fields, so a run that quietly lost a mechanism cannot keep
   reproducing a stale digest.
3. **Migration is real, bounded and double-entered.** Permanent movers take people, food, silver
   and movable property and abandon the fields they were working (a named outflow: nobody receives
   that land). Seasonal migrants leave for a declared term, eat where they arrive, and come home
   when the term ends or the money runs out. Movers to a boundary node leave the modelled
   population, recorded as an exit. Every movement is a logged transition on both sides, and the
   invariants rebuild households, adults, grain, silver and land from the log alone.
4. **A county is the first choice, the exit the last resort.** Destination choice is one declared
   comparison — the reachable county with the lowest posted price, ties by node id — with two
   fallbacks that exist because boundary nodes post the reference price and never respond to
   scarcity. Left to a plain price comparison, the region emptied into the exits and *internal*
   migration never happened at all; that finding is why the rule reads the way it does.
5. **Migration and the military are opt-in.** `with_migration` and `with_military` default to
   false, so the P03–P05 pinned baselines are exactly what they were; the sandbox turns both on.
6. **Governance indicators are a table, never an index.** Eight measured quantities, each against a
   declared reading line, with the count of crossed lines reported as a count of rows. No aggregate
   score exists in the code, and the thresholds are parameters graded `S`, not evidence.
7. **Analysis reads the log.** Every metric is derived from events, with the detailed frames
   (distress by class and node, flows, roster, tax base, dispersion) written beside the summary so
   any number can be traced to what produced it.

What the surface shows, in the model's own terms:

- **Migration needs distress to start, and then it moves one node downstream.** The calm runs move
  nobody at all (nobody meets P03's eligibility rule). In the shocked runs 21 households leave
  toy-sx-a for toy-sx-b — the cheapest reachable county — and on the medium fixture 21 leave
  medium-sx-a, of which 10.3 settle at medium-sx-b and 10.7 leave the region entirely. Internal and
  exit migration are reported separately for exactly this reason.
- **Only households that can pay their own way move.** The gate is silver per household against the
  declared cost of a move (0.5 tael), so the very poorest cohorts — the landless and the tenants,
  with half a tael per household in the toy fixture — cannot leave at all. Whether that is a
  finding or an artifact of the fixture's liquidity is a P09 question; here it is a declared rule
  and a visible number.
- **Temporary migration is the larger human flow by two orders of magnitude.** ~1,950 adults went
  away for a season in each shocked run against 21 households moving for good: in this model the
  ladder's middle rungs carry far more people than its top rung, which is what the coping ladder
  implies and what P03 could not show.
- **Prices disperse with the shock** (coefficient of variation 0.02 → 0.45), and dispersion is the
  signal migration reads.
- **The climate, not the county count, drives the armed sector**: band strength 2,551 → 3,992 (toy)
  and 5,783 → 7,643 (medium) between calm and shock, while the medium fixture's extra counties add
  bands roughly in proportion to nodes.
- **Five of eight indicators cross in every scenario, including the calm ones.** That is not a
  shock result: at this fiscal scale the county never collects half its assessed quota, arrears
  always grow, the garrison's monthly pay is always short, armed men exceed one percent of adults
  somewhere in the run, and the visible tax base contracts. P06 reported the same structural
  feature for pay and arrears. It is a statement about the toy county's scale, and it is recorded
  rather than tuned away. The first draft of this phase could not have reported the fifth: the
  indicator compared a single county-tick minimum against a sum of final bases and was structurally
  incapable of firing — the independent review caught it (M1 below), and the corrected measure
  reads 0.0395 against a 0.02 line.

## Independent review and disposition

A read-only reviewer went over the whole diff — scheduler, migration, analysis, experiment, tests
and docs — and returned **approve with follow-ups**: no blockers, three major findings, eight minor
ones. All eleven were fixed in this phase rather than deferred, because each one either falsified a
claim or hid a rule.

| id | finding | disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M1 | `tax_base_contraction` compared the smallest single county-tick against a sum of final bases, so it measured an aggregation mistake and could never fire | Both ends are now the same aggregation (sum of firsts against sum of lasts); the indicator reads 0.0395 and crosses |
| M2 | The armed-share indicator used the starting adult population in the governance artifact and the end population in the metric row, so the two could disagree | One denominator (starting adults) in both call sites; the integration test would now catch a divergence |

## What this phase found (defects fixed here)

1. **The sandbox was not reproducible across processes.** Two runs of the same scenario produced
   99,703 and 99,821 events. The cause was iterating a `set` of node ids when deciding which bands
   and garrisons to serve each tick: string hashing is randomised per process, so the *number* of
   events depended on the interpreter's hash seed. Five such iterations existed (band recruitment,
   the consumption and fiscal market maps, the labour-demand map). All are sorted now, and the
   pins are hash-seed independent. Nothing short of a cross-process check could have caught it —
   the in-process replay tests had been green for six phases.
2. **Nothing would ever have migrated.** The permanent-move rule multiplied the migration edge's
   capacity by the monthly share, double-discounting a quantity P02 already declares in households
   per month: at the toy scale the cap came out at 2.4 households against a declared minimum move
   of 5, so the rule could never fire.
3. **Every mover would have left the region.** With destinations chosen by posted price alone, and
   boundary nodes posting a constant reference price, the exit always won once any county was even
   slightly scarce: internal migration was structurally impossible until the county-first rule was
   written.
4. **A run with no migration crashed the report.** `node_migration` concatenated three possibly
   empty frames; a calm window with no movers raised `cannot concat empty list`.
5. **Migration would have rewritten P03–P05's recorded results.** Wiring it by default changed the
   household, market and fiscal baselines (event counts and digests). It is now opt-in, like the
   military, and the earlier pins are untouched.

## Open items for P08 and later

- **Land abandoned is not reallocated.** Fields a migrant household leaves simply leave the model:
  no neighbour, elite or newcomer takes them up, so out-migration shrinks the tax base without
  concentrating land. The land-concentration metric therefore cannot respond to migration yet.
- **Arrivals bring no land**, and there is no tenancy market: incomers are landless in their new
  catchment with no rule that says who employs them.
- **Temporary migrants consume at the destination but do not work there.** Their food is bought and
  eaten; no wage, no remittance, so a season away costs silver and buys survival, never savings.
- **The four structural indicators cross in calm runs too.** Until P08/P09 put a sourced fiscal
  base under the toy county, the governance indicators are reading the fixture's scale.
- **A market trade is now attributed to the phase that asked for it** — a ration purchase under
  `military_finance`, a household's dinner under `household_consumption` — which re-pinned the P04
  severe market baseline and the P05 fiscal baseline for instrumentation only. Verified by an A/B
  run: identical sequence numbers, identical event count, every column byte-identical except the
  `phase` token on 600 merchant rows. The calm market baseline is untouched.
- **The garrison starves before it is paid off.** Relief (phase 08) draws grain before the garrison
  (phase 10), so the military food channel does most of the work in the desertion rate; the tick
  order is declared and diagrammed, and P10's ablations are the right instrument to separate the
  two channels.
- **Band activity still does not disturb trade.** `TradeDisruption` remains unwired, and no
  mechanism responds to violence except suppression.
- **Migration is not yet a strategy.** Destination choice is one price comparison: no kin, no
  information about conditions two links away, no return of permanent migrants, no household
  splitting beyond the seasonal rung.
- **The poorest cannot afford to move.** The move costs 0.5 tael per household and is gated on
  silver per household, so cohorts below that line stay however distressed they are. The gate is
  honest but the cost is a declared assumption; P09 should calibrate it against something.
- **The tax base contracts everywhere, calm included** (12,758 mu on the toy fixture, 30,748 on the
  medium one), which is elite accumulation plus migration abandonment rather than extraction
  pressure. The indicator now measures it correctly, but nothing yet separates the two channels.

## Changes to earlier phases

- `HouseholdCohortAgent.check_balances` no longer asserts that a cohort's weight never changes: with
  households now moving, weight is a tracked balance reconciled against its own ledger key
  (`households_delta_households`), and only migration and the two migration primitives can move it.
- The `System` protocol gained `reads`/`writes`; every existing system was annotated without any
  behavioural change. `SimulationKernel` uses `core.scheduler.validate`, and `RunManifest` gained
  two required fields, so any test or tool that constructs a manifest directly had to be updated.
- Five `set` iterations in P03–P06 systems were sorted. No rule changed; the runs became
  process-independent, which is why the pins taken before this phase remain valid.
- `networks/fixtures.py` helpers now take an explicit provenance argument, so no dataset can
  inherit another's provenance by omission. The toy fixture's values are unchanged.
