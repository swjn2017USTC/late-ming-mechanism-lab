## Contract decisions

1. **The selection is a rule with an accounting, not a list.** Candidates are CHGIS seats in
   present-day Shaanxi and Henan; the filters are seat type, coordinates, a CHGIS date range covering
   the window, a declared agrarian zone, at least five recorded events within 60 km, and a courier
   station within 40 km. Every seat each filter removes is counted, and the counts reconcile to the
   candidate total exactly — an invariant test asserts it, because the first draft was off by one (a
   CHGIS row with a null seat type).
2. **The sample is grown, not spread.** Per province, the best-covered eligible seat seeds the chain
   and each step adds the best-covered reachable seat within 80 km, at least 25 km away, until the
   province's quota (proportional to its eligible seats, minimum 3) is met. The rule's limitation is
   serialised with the dataset: it selects for record coverage and proximity, and makes no claim that
   these were the important places.
3. **Proximity is a candidate, never a link.** The candidate pool is every located pair within 150 km,
   graded `S`. Each graph then accepts on its own rule: the documented courier chain for military
   (grade `B`), its `MAJ_MINOR = 1` subset for trade (grade `C`, the grain movement inferred), range
   for migration (grade `S`). An accepted pair that is not a candidate is a connector by definition,
   and the report says how many.
4. **Connectors are labelled and counted.** The documented rules alone do not connect a twelve-seat
   two-province sample, so each graph is completed with the minimum spanning tree over its permitted
   nodes. A connector is graded `S`, its note begins `Connector`, and the coverage document prints
   the documented/connector split per graph — military 10/4, trade 7/7, migration 7/7 — so the share
   of each graph that is evidence is visible rather than implied.
5. **The repository stores the annual observation, never a monthly one.** `climate-annual.parquet`
   holds per-node-year counts and the declared index. The monthly series is computed at run time by
   `AllocatedObservedClimate` from a parameter-carded profile, so the allocator can be ablated (the
   uniform mode) and swept (V2-P07) without a stored "observation" existing to be mistaken for data.
6. **A node-year the record does not cover is a decision, not a zero.** The strict policy refuses and
   names the exact node-periods; the historical scenario declares imputation and every imputed
   node-year is counted in the manifest and the run sidecar. Here 56 of 240 node-years (23.3%) are
   imputed, and 190 of 2,880 allocated node-months clip at the ceiling — both measured, both in the
   provenance.
7. **Boundary exits are window seats too.** A gateway node is the nearest located seat of the
   neighbouring province whose CHGIS date range covers 1625–1644, and its note states its own dates.
   The Sichuan corridor is not built and the reason is recorded: it runs through the Han valley,
   which is outside the two declared agrarian zones.
8. **Historical runs go somewhere else.** `outputs/v2/historical-core-v1/` is a V2 root; the V1
   artifact tree is untouched, and an integration test asserts it. Each run writes
   `v2-provenance.json` beside the V1 manifest, carrying the dataset, the source hashes, the allocator
   mode and the imputation count, because the V1 manifest does not know about any of them.
9. **The new parameter set is not a sandbox parameter.** `HistoricalCoreParameters` is consumed by
   the dataset build and the allocator, not by a parameter model the economy holds. A declared
   exclusion in `sensitivity.NON_ECONOMY_PARAMETER_SETS` keeps it out of the sandbox sweep, with the
   reason and the V2-P07 hand-off recorded; without it, adding the cards silently changed every
   Morris/Sobol design, the batch families and the synthesis bundle.

## What the core is

```text
dataset        historical-core-v1, schema historical-core-v1
candidates     10522 CHGIS seats -> 95 eligible -> 72 on the documented network -> 12 selected
nodes          12 county seats (Shaanxi 4, Henan 8) + 2 boundary exits
               (Licheng Xian, Shanxi; Shangjin Xian, Huguang), all grade B, all on the courier network
window         1625-1644, 240 node-years, of which 184 carry a record and 56 are imputed zeros
climate        annual index per node-year from REACHES counts (drought 30, famine 35, crop 33, pest 32),
               peaked 1639-1641; allocated monthly by the recorded seasonal profile
graphs         trade 14 links (7 documented), migration 14 (7), military 14 (10)
run            240 ticks, 226,470 events, 9.4 s, digest c75cbc96…, replay identical
```

The observed forcing now concentrates the crisis where the record does — 1639 to 1641 — which is what
the V1 stationary synthetic climate could not produce, and the reason three hold-out patterns failed
in P09.

## Independent review

An independent reviewer agent (`V2P02Review`, read-only) recomputed the selection ledger from the raw
layer, rebuilt all 240 climate rows, recomputed the monthly profile, verified the hop structure of
every documented military link on a rebuilt station graph, re-ran the build in memory and the runs,
and checked the rights and the digests. Verdict: **approve with follow-ups**, no blockers. Findings
and dispositions:

| # | severity | finding | disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **MAJOR** | Both boundary exits came from CHGIS seats whose existence ranges exclude the window by a millennium (596–605 and 420–478), and their notes repeated the core's "covers 1625-1644" boilerplate | Fixed: exit candidates are filtered to seats whose range covers the window, so the exits are now Licheng Xian (1086–1911, Shanxi) and Shangjin Xian (1380–1658, Huguang); each note states its own dates, the report names the exits, and a test asserts it |
| 2 | **MAJOR** | The exclusion ledger was off by one: a CHGIS row with a null seat type was filtered but not counted (103 against a true 104), so "every exclusion is counted" was false | Fixed: null seat types are counted explicitly, and `selection_report` now carries `candidate_filters_account_for_candidates`, asserted by a test. The ledger reconciles exactly |
| 3 | MINOR | `SelectionReport.exits` was never populated | Fixed: the exits are recorded and printed in the coverage document |
| 4 | MINOR | The module docstring described the earlier farthest-point rule | Fixed: the docstring now states the implemented growth rule |
| 5 | MINOR | The allocator's "annual mean is preserved" claim is false where months clip at one, and the worst years are exactly the ones that clip | Fixed: the docstring states the limit, and `clipped_node_months` measures it (190 of 2,880) and enters the run provenance |
| 6 | MINOR | A trade link's note called a mixed-class chain a chain of major segments | Fixed: the note now says the chain's *strongest* segment is major and that a chain may include a minor leg |
| 7 | MINOR | The migration distances were attributed to the courier layer in the field table | Fixed: migration distances cite the CHGIS county points they are computed from |

The reviewer verified as holding: the selection ledger (every count reproduced independently), the
per-field provenance (no declared value graded `B`, no sourced value graded `S`), the whole climate
chain row by row, the monthly profile to four decimals, the documented/connector split and the 1–2 hop
structure of all ten documented military links, the rights position (`data/raw` untracked, digests
verify, `derived_committed` matches what was published), and the pinned run digest. It could not
certify clause-level EULA legality (it read the snapshot statements, not the licence PDFs) and flags
one accepted risk: aggregated climate output is published while the REACHES licence remains `unknown`
— which is the rule V2-P01 set, and V2-P02 keeps the aggregate-only constraint.

## Open items for V2-P03 and later

- **23.3% of node-years are imputed zeros**, because the record does not cover every seat in every
  year. Whether the model should impute, refuse, or carry the last observation is V2-P03's decision,
  and the count is in every run's provenance until then.
- **The graphs are half documented**: military 10 of 14 links, trade and migration 7 of 14. The
  connectors are labelled, but the documented share is the number V2-P04 should move.
- **The observed index is an event count, not a severity.** The weights and saturation counts are
  declared; V2-P07 should sweep the whole allocator, and the profile as one distribution rather than
  twelve numbers.
- **The Sichuan corridor is out of scope** because the Han valley is outside the two declared agrarian
  zones. Adding a third zone would reopen it; nothing else does.
- **`parameter_hash` is still `None`** in the V1 run manifest; the V2 sidecar carries the forcing
  provenance instead, and wiring the kernel manifest remains open from P09.
- **Only 12 seats were selected** of 72 eligible. Widening the core is a data question (more zones,
  denser coverage), not a parameter.
