# V2-P05 — Missing Mechanism Variants

Status: complete. Run against `docs/exec-plans/V2-P05.md`. No V2-P06 work started.

## What was done

Five of the six V1 cards rested on a structure the model lacked or had never exercised. The phase
builds the variants that can decide them, runs them on the historical core at V2-P04's declared seeds,
and reports one card as still undecidable rather than inventing the rule that would decide it.

### M006 — a mortality interface, and no mortality rule

The card is `UNIDENTIFIED`; the phase keeps it there, and the evidence says why:

- `pop-ming-qing-decline` is grade **C**, asserts a **direction**, and `supports_parameters: []`.
- The famine cluster's own note: "no source in this registry measures a mortality or unmet-need
  series at county resolution."

Delivered instead: `src/late_ming_lab/systems/mortality.py` (the `MortalityRule` interface, the
declared `NoMortality` the model actually runs, `ShortfallObservable`, and a
`mortality_rule_from_evidence` that **refuses** to build a rule from a direction); the observables
recomputed from a run (`src/late_ming_lab/analysis/mortality.py`); and a gap report
(`docs/v2/mortality-gap.md`). `apply()` refuses any rule that returns deaths, because no transition in
the population layer could carry them.

What a rule would see, on the reference run: 60 cohorts observed over 240 months, **16 with a
shortfall**, the longest run below the floor **48 months**, rolling unmet ratio max **0.2245**, deaths
**0.0**. The observables exist; nothing outside the model exists to calibrate a rate against.

`NoMortality` is **not an ablation** — the card's own argument, and the phase's: an arm whose
intervention cannot bind measures the absence of a rule, the mistake `NO_BAND_MERGER` made.

### M005 — concentration attributed, and the merger link finally exercised

`analysis/band_chain.py` attributes the change in the largest band's share to six components
(formation, refugee intake, deserter intake, merger, split, dissolution) as an exact partition: the
per-tick steps telescope into the recorded series, and a movement whose cause token is unknown is
refused rather than dropped.

The card recorded **zero merges in every P10 arm** and never exercised the link. Both controls were
run on the historical core:

| arm | merger troops | largest band's share at end | bands at end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `band-merge-negative-control` (bar 1.0) | **0.0** | 0.1994 | 7 |
| reference (bar 0.6) | 582.8 | 0.2386 | 7 |
| `band-merge-positive-control` (bar 0.0) | **4,094.3** | **0.3405** | 6 |

The card's own falsifier is therefore answered: **merges fire when the bar allows, and the largest
band's share rises with them** (0.2386 → 0.3405, bands 7 → 6). The merger link is not decorative on
this input and on this window; the card's uncertainty was about the P10 arms, and it is resolved here
for the historical core. Co-location is not the obstacle: 106 node-months hold two or more bands, up
to four in one month.

### M001/M002 — policy against structure

The minimal factorial, one seed, four cells:

| cell | inversion present | breakdown reached | receipts over quota | base change (mu) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| escalating × inventory-price | **1** | 0 | 0.3983 | −27,777.0 |
| escalating × demand-pressure-price | **1** | 0 | 0.3978 | −27,703.9 |
| fixed × inventory-price | **0** | 0 | 0.3292 | −27,777.0 |

The inversion reading follows the **policy** and not the price structure: it is present in both

not exercised by this design — which is reported rather than worked around.

`tests/invariants/test_mass_balance.py` (7 invariants) checks grain, silver, land, population, labour

## Changes to the model, and the controls that hold them

Each new rule is neutral at the value that reproduces V1, so the ablation arm is a no-op **by
construction** and the detector refuses it if it is not: `arrears-rules-off` and
`elite-mediation-only` both report "reproduced the reference" at every declared seed.

Two structural decisions worth naming, both enforced by tests and both commented at the call site:

- **Recovery and remission run before the liquidation ladder.** Placed after it, recovery never fired
  at any threshold, because the ladder spends a household's silver on the current assessment first.
  Collecting old obligations before the month's own assessment is the rule.
- **Settlement runs after the silver payment and before the ladder.** A settlement is a discount *on
  a payment*: the first version credited a share of the outstanding obligation whatever the household
  had paid, and the reviewer measured it firing on cohorts holding no silver at all — 4,224 of 11,364
  rows, 32% of what it removed. That was an assessment haircut wearing a settlement's name, and the
  corrected rule removes 83.5 tael where the wrong one removed 70,932.

## Independent review

Verdict: **blocked then fixed** — one blocker and four majors, all addressed before the commit.

- **BLOCKER — the generated document misreported its own measurements.** The band and arrears tables
  read chain keys nothing writes (`band_chain_formation`, `largest_share_max`,
  `tax_arrears_relief_tael`), so the phase's human-facing M005 and M003 readings printed zeros beside
  4,094 merged troops and eleven thousand relief events. Fixed: the tables read the keys the readers
  actually write and sum the relief tael from the log; the loader now recomputes the band chain rather
  than dropping it. The reviewer independently verified the attribution's partition is exact (the six
  components sum to the recorded change, 0.34050739, to 1e-14).
- **MAJOR — the settlement rule did not bind as declared** (the haircut above). Fixed, and the card's
  wording is now what the code does.
- **MAJOR — the report's M006 observables were from another run.** Corrected to the shipped
  artifact's numbers (16 cohorts, 0.2245).
- **MAJOR — the factorial's breakdown reading passed zero adults and a row count as its
  denominators**, pinning two of the eight reading lines shut. Fixed; the reviewer re-ran the cell
  with the corrected inputs and the conclusion holds (breakdown still never reached, 4 of 8 crossed
  against 3 under the broken reading), so the corrected measurement is reported.
- **MAJOR (open, carried)** — the recovery rule's placement is a disclosed device required for it to
  bind, and no card records the collection order. The order is now stated in the code and exercised by
  a test; recording it on the card is V2-P06's, and it is listed below.

## Open items for V2-P06 and later

- **M006 needs a series, not an argument.** The interface and the observables are in place; the
  missing piece is a county-resolution mortality or unmet-need series with a level and a range.
- **The recovery rule fires, and on a threshold the phase chose.** Its placement is defensible from
  the code, but the "half a month of assessment" line is an assumption like the others, and it enters
  the sensitivity pool.
- **M002's conditionality is still unmeasured**: no factorial cell reaches the breakdown reading on
  the historical core, so the design says nothing about the gate there. A cell that reaches it needs a
  lever that moves the county's own finances, not the price rule.
- **The band result is one window and one input.** Merges fire on the historical core at four seeds;
  whether the same holds on the P10 fixtures is not something this phase re-ran.
- **The elite branch is measured on one declared term and one share.** Both are grade `S`, and the
  base-contraction comparison the M004 card asks for is reported for the historical core only.
