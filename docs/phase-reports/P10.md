# P10 — Ablation / Sensitivity / Counterfactual

Date: 2026-09-14
Status: complete. No P11 work started.

## Scope

Turn the model on itself: what is a mechanism *for*, and which parameters does the model's behaviour
actually depend on? Three experiments, all on the P07 sandbox under common random numbers — an
ablation set of nine named mechanisms plus three two-way arms, a Morris screen followed by a Sobol
design with second-order indices, and a two-parameter grid for the region rather than the curve —
and four generated reports that carry distributions rather than means. The scope document is
`docs/exec-plans/P10.md`; this report is what came out.

### Acceptance criteria

```text
experiment runner · the nine named ablations runnable as declared differences from one baseline ·
Morris then Sobol, with second-order indices for interactions · common random numbers across arms
and across design rows · distributions reported, not means (quantiles, paired intervals, effect
sizes) · collapse probability with its interval · tax-base trajectory, migration distribution,
market connectivity, military arrears, largest band share, time-to-governance-breakdown ·
interaction effects · tipping regions · non-linearities · no USTC LLM call · provenance per batch
```

### Non-goals

No mechanism, rule or parameter value changed: the two P07 switches the sandbox leaves off —
extraction escalation and trade disruption — are turned on *in the declared baseline arm* rather
than by editing the model, and every arm is a computed difference from that baseline. No historical
fitting (that was P09), no calibration re-run, no regional scale, no UI, no runtime LLM.

## Contract decisions

1. **One baseline, nine computed differences.** `BASELINE` is the single declaration of everything an
   arm may vary; each ablation is a function from it, and `configuration_diff` computes what an arm
   changes. A report therefore cannot claim a change an arm does not make, or miss one it does. A
   test pins each arm's changed-field set.
2. **Two of the nine ablations needed a baseline that switches the mechanism on.** The P07 sandbox
   runs a fixed extraction policy and calm trade, so `NO_EXTRACTION_ESCALATION` and
   `NO_TRADE_DISRUPTION` would have been no-ops against it. The declared P10 baseline therefore uses
   the escalating policy the P05 experiment already declared
   (`ArrearsEscalation(0.5, arrears_weight=0.5, ceiling=1.0)`) and a declared disruption regime
   (`ScaledDisruption(risk_scale=1.5, capacity_scale=0.5)` — the smallest round manipulation that is
   plainly not the identity). Both are scenario declarations, both are printed in the report, and
   the model still has **no endogenous link from armed activity to transport**.
3. **Common random numbers, twice over.** Replicate *r* of every arm uses root seed `base_seed + r`,
   so a paired difference is the intervention rather than a fresh weather sequence; design rows keep
   replicate 0, so variation across a Morris, Sobol or grid row is the parameters and nothing else.
   A test asserts the arm pairing and the manifest records the rule.
4. **Distributions, not means.** Every comparison carries medians and quartiles per arm, the paired
   median difference with a percentile bootstrap interval, the share of replicates that move the same
   way, and Cliff's delta; a direction is claimed only when the interval excludes zero, and the rest
   is reported as *unresolved*. Collapse probability is a share with a Wilson interval.
5. **The breakdown line is declared and its cost is measured.** A run is *in breakdown* when at least
   six of the eight governance reading lines are crossed at a sampled tick — P07 measured five
   crossing even in calm runs, so the sixth is the earliest line that separates a crisis run from its
   own baseline. Because that is a reading rule rather than evidence, the report prints the
   distribution of crossed lines and recomputes the share at every candidate line from three to
   eight.
6. **Market connectivity had no measure, so one is defined.** From the declared trade graph and the
   `TRADE_SHIPMENT` events: the share of edges that carried traffic, and the share of nodes in the
   largest component of the subgraph the active edges induce. Both are reported; neither is a claim
   about a historical network.
7. **The Sobol set is what the batch ran, not what the rule would choose now.** The report reads the
   selection recorded in the batch manifest, because SALib's indices are only meaningful against the
   design they came from. Sensitivity outputs are four: crossed lines, largest band share, tax-base
   change and households departed.
8. **A degenerate output is reported, not crashed on and not called zero.** SALib divides by a
   slice's variance; when a slice is flat the estimator is undefined, and the analysis returns NaN
   rows with NaN intervals rather than a confident zero. Two regression tests pin that (a constant
   output, and an output whose A and B slices are constant while the rest varies).

## What this phase found (and fixed)

1. **A batch that prints only at the end is a batch nobody can watch.** The first attempts at the
   real run were killed after twenty-odd minutes with nothing written. The runner now takes a
   `progress` callback, the entry point prints a line per run, and a stall is visible as a stall.
2. **The compute budget was wrong three times, and each correction is recorded here rather than
   quietly absorbed.** The baseline arm runs with escalation and disruption on, which makes a
   240-tick run ~7.6 s instead of the calibration batch's ~4.3 s; the first design (12 replicates,
   24 Sobol base samples, a 5×5 grid) would have taken hours. Then the machine itself turned out to
   be the binding constraint: with several other sessions resident, load average ran at 8–11 on ten
   cores, a run cost 12–30 s, and four parallel workers thrashed memory (38 % free, 1.9 M pageouts)
   rather than helping. The batch that ran is the largest one this machine could finish: 4
   replicates per arm, 2 Morris trajectories, 8 Sobol base samples over 4 parameters, a 3×3 grid
   with one run per cell, 2 workers. Every interval in the reports is printed precisely so a reader
   can see what that size buys.
3. **The Sobol analysis was fragile to flat outputs, and the guard had to mirror SALib's layout.**
   SALib splits the design into *strided* slices (`A = Y[0::step]`, `B = Y[step-1::step]`,
   `AB[:, j] = Y[j+1::step]`), not contiguous blocks, and returns a one-element array where a scalar
   is expected when a slice has no variance. The first guard checked contiguous blocks and missed the
   real case; the second mirrors the sampler's layout and is pinned by a test that reproduces the
   crash.
4. **The review found a real blocker in the statistics** (see the review section below): the sentinel
   that marks "never broke down" was entering the paired comparisons as if it were a time, which
   would have printed a baseline *median time to breakdown* of −1 ticks and, worse, inverted the
   direction whenever one arm broke down and the other never did. The sentinel is now kept out of
   every arithmetic operation and the timing question is answered by a conditional summary.

## Ablation: which mechanisms the crisis needs

The baseline breaks down — six of the eight declared governance lines crossed — in none of its four
replicates: it peaks at four or five lines (`peak_at_least_5` in 1 of 4). So the binary collapse
probability is a weak discriminator at this sample size, and the report leans on the crossed-line
distribution and the continuous outputs, as `ablation.md` prints them.

**The one arm that reaches the line is the one that opens the migration gate.** `OPEN_MIGRATION_EXIT`
breaks down in **4 of 4 replicates**, and it does so through the fiscal channel rather than by any
direct rule:

| metric | baseline median | arm median | paired difference (95 % interval) | direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| households exited | 0 | 8,679 | +8,515 (3,896 … 9,733) | up |
| households departed | 534 | 73,495 | +72,947 (71,545 … 87,513) | up |
| tax base change (mu) | −15,476 | −178,043 | −162,553 (−170,826 … −161,633) | down |
| military pay arrears, end (tael) | 15,751 | 25,738 | +10,566 (7,290 … 14,415) | up |
| receipts over quota | 0.267 | 0.354 | +0.076 (0.019 … 0.125) | up |

The other two resolved increases in crossings are not: removing the drought leaves 5 lines crossed
in every run (against 1 in 4 for the baseline), which is P07's "the structural indicators cross in
calm runs too" showing up as a measurement rather than a footnote.

The remaining arms move the outputs their own mechanism reaches, and several move them the *wrong
way* from the naive expectation — which is the point of an ablation:

- **`FULL_MILITARY_PAY` raises arrears** (+2,300 tael, interval 1,555…4,753): giving the pay claim
  the whole treasury does not clear it, because the claim is larger than any month's silver and the
  arrears stock keeps growing; paying more also buys a larger band share and a larger tax-base
  contraction. The army's crisis is fiscal, not one of priority.
- **`NO_DROUGHT` empties the migration flow** (departures 534 → 0) and *reduces market activity*
  (active trade links 0.92 → 0.33): with no shocks, posted prices equalise and there is far less to
  arbitrage. Fewer, not more, shipments is the model's answer here.
- **`NO_ELITE_CREDIT` deepens the tax-base contraction** (−23,960 against −15,476) but slightly
  *raises* receipts over quota: without credit, more land is sold to pay tax, so more of the base is
  visible in the years it is sold.
- **`NO_TRADE_DISRUPTION` is the only arm whose tax base contracts less** (+1,278 mu on the change),
  and it lowers receipts over quota by 0.042.
- **`LOW_REPRESSION`** reduces the largest band's share (0.40 → 0.35) and raises departures (+73):
  the coercive channel holds armed concentration down and keeps some households in place.
- **`NO_ELITE_CREDIT` and `LOW_REPRESSION`** each move departures up; both remove a buffer.

### Did the ablations actually bind?

The counters make that a measurement, not a claim (`ablation.md` prints the table):

| mechanism | counter | baseline | arm | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `NO_ELITE_CREDIT` | `elite_loans` | 74 | 0 | removed |
| `LOW_REPRESSION` | `suppressions` | 936.5 | 0 | removed |
| `NO_BAND_MERGER` | `band_merges` | 0 | 0 | **nothing to remove**: no band merged in any of the four baseline replicates, so this arm is a structural no-op at these seeds and its null results are uninformative |
| `NO_TRADE_DISRUPTION` | `trade_shipments` | 318.5 | 249.5 | the regime changed; the count *fell*, because calm trade removes the price gaps that motivate shipment |

The `band_merges` row is exactly the kind of reading the counter exists for. A reviewer predicted it
from the mechanics (merging needs two bands at the cohesion ceiling, which the model reaches
rarely), the counter confirmed it on the artifact, and the phase reports the arm as uninformative at
these seeds rather than as evidence that merging does not matter.

## Interactions

`interactions.md` compares each joint arm's paired difference with the sum of its two single-arm
differences, with a bootstrap interval on the gap. The results are in the artifact; the reading is
that at four replicates the interval is wide, so most cells come back *additive* — which the report
prints as "not visible at this sample size" rather than as independence.

## Sensitivity: the ranking is readable, the Sobol indices are not

The Morris screen (2 trajectories × 15 parameters = 32 runs) ranks the parameters by their effect on
each output. On the governance crossing count — the phase's primary output — the ranking is:

| parameter | mu | mu* | sigma |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `temporary_share_of_adults_per_month` | +0.75 | 2.25 | 3.18 |
| `reference_price_tael_per_shi` | 0 | 1.50 | 2.12 |
| `land_per_adult_capacity_mu`, `assessed_value_tael_per_mu`, `subsistence_grain_per_adult_month_shi`, `food_shi_per_soldier_month`, `pay_tael_per_soldier_month` | −0.75 | 0.75 | 1.06 |
| the remaining eight | 0 | 0 | 0 |

A μ* of zero means the parameter did not move the crossed-line count in either trajectory — with two
trajectories that is a screen in name only, and the report prints σ beside μ* so a reader can see
that most of these rankings rest on one or two elementary effects. On the migration output the same
design does separate parameters more convincingly (`subsistence_grain_per_adult_month_shi` μ* ≈ 2.3 ×
10⁴, `permanent_share_of_households_per_month` 2.1 × 10⁴, `permanent_migration_unmet_ratio` 8.6 ×
10³), which is where the design has room to work: departures are a large, continuous quantity.

**The Sobol batch is reported as a failed attempt.** At 8 base samples (40 runs) over the four
selected parameters, 4 of 16 first-order indices are undefined — the crossed-line count is a discrete
outcome and does not vary within the design's strided slices, so SALib's estimator divides by zero
and the analysis reports NaN rather than a confident zero — and 7 of the remaining estimates lie
outside the [0, 1] a variance share can take, with most intervals covering zero. Indices like
`assessed_value_tael_per_mu` S1 = 3.5 on the largest band share are not a large effect; they are an
estimator with no resolving power at four base samples. The report states the counts and the
conclusion; the phase's sensitivity finding is the Morris ranking and the response curves, and the
Sobol design needs a budget this machine did not have.

## Tipping: one cell, and it is one run

The grid (3 × 3 over `temporary_share_of_adults_per_month` × `reference_price_tael_per_shi`, one run
per cell) shows a single breakdown — the low-absence, cheap-grain corner — and 5 crossed lines
everywhere else. With one run per cell that corner is one run, not a probability, and the response
tables are flat because the crossed-line count is discrete and 5 is the modal value. The honest
reading is that **this grid cannot resolve a tipping region at one run per cell**: a region needs
enough runs per cell to estimate a share, and the batch did not have them. What the grid does show is
that the two parameters the Morris screen ranks highest do not produce a visible surface on the
discrete outcome at this resolution — which is why the phase's `line_sensitivity` table, at a lower
line, is the place a region shows up at all (the `OPEN_MIGRATION_EXIT` arm sits at 6 of 8 while
every other arm sits at 4–5).

## Review

An independent reviewer agent read the change set before commit and reported 13 findings — 1
blocker, 2 major, 10 minor — of which every one was fixed rather than argued with:

| # | severity | finding | disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **BLOCKER** | the −1 "never broke down" sentinel entered the paired statistics: the baseline's *median time to breakdown* would have printed as −1 ticks, and the direction would have inverted whenever one arm broke down and the other never did | the sentinel is out of every paired metric; a new `time_to_breakdown_summary` reports the timing conditioned on the runs that reached the line, with the never-count beside it; a test pins the exclusion |
| 2 | MAJOR | the sensitivity report said the selection rule was "capped at six" while the code caps at four | the text now prints the constant |
| 3 | MAJOR | the `NO_BAND_MERGER` note claimed cohesion 1.0 is unreachable, but the clamp makes exactly 1.0 reachable for a fully fed band in a no-intake month | the note now states the exact condition and says whether the arm binds is a measurement here, not a proof |
| 4 | MINOR | `NO_BAND_MERGER` is a structural no-op in most replicates and the report did not say so | mechanism counters were added (`band_merges`, `elite_loans`, `suppressions`, `trade_shipments`) and `ablation.md` prints a binding table; the counters confirmed the reviewer's prediction exactly (0 merges in the baseline's four replicates) |
| 5 | MINOR | `tipping_report` recomputed the Morris ranking with hardcoded levels and seed, and never checked the axes it printed against the batch | it reads the manifest's design and prints the recorded axes |
| 6 | MINOR | `receipts_over_quota_end` is a whole-window ratio, not an end state | renamed `receipts_over_quota_total` |
| 7 | MINOR | the grid's response curve binned four sampled values into equal-width bins, mislabelling the centres and scaling the curvature | `response_by_value` reads a grid at its own values; the bin-centre version stays for sampled designs |
| 8 | MINOR | `governance_timeline` sampled the ticks that carried events, not the calendar | it samples `range(0, last_tick + 1, every)`, so two runs are compared on the same grid |
| 9 | MINOR | `morris_indices` had no length guard while `sobol_indices` did | the same fail-closed check was added |
| 10 | MINOR | the bootstrap settings were not in the manifest | recorded as `statistics: {resamples, confidence, seed}` |
| 11 | MINOR | the governance timeline was computed twice per run | the runner computes it once and passes it in |
| 12 | MINOR | `response_curve`'s bins were closed on both edges, so a value on an interior edge counted twice | half-open bins, last bin closed |
| 13 | MINOR | the `-1` sentinel's readers were not all checked for consistency | the timing summary, the collapse share and `line_sensitivity` now handle it explicitly, and the report says which quantities it may never enter |

The reviewer's own probes are part of the evidence: it ran baseline replicates at the real seeds to
establish that merges occur in only two of eight, which is what made finding 4 concrete.

The invariants the suite enforces, in the words of the tests:

- the nine ablations the plan names are exactly the declared ones, and each changes only the fields
  it declares (the difference is computed from the configuration, not written down twice);
- two batches' arms share a root seed at the same replicate, and a replicate's seed changes with the
  replicate — common random numbers, asserted on the artifact;
- a design row applies only its own parameter columns, and a grid covers the Cartesian product;
- the paired comparison aligns on the replicate, refuses arms that do not share them, and calls a
  difference only when its interval excludes zero;
- the interaction statistic is the joint effect minus the sum of the parts, and its verdict is
  additive when the interval covers zero;
- a straight response reads as zero curvature and a step reads as a spike;
- `breakdown` is 1 exactly when the timing sentinel is not, and the timing summary keeps the
  sentinel out of its quantiles;
- the declared breakdown line is above the calm baseline P07 measured, and is one of the lines the
  report recomputes;
- a batch writes its runs, its governed timeline and a manifest that names every arm, its computed
  differences, the design and the statistics settings;
- the pipeline test runs one tiny batch of each kind and checks that the reports it generates read
  the artifacts they name, including a report number recomputed from the table it claims to describe.

## Open items for P11 and later

- **The batch is sized by the machine, not by the question.** Four replicates pair the arms but
  cannot resolve a small effect; the Sobol design cannot estimate a variance share at all at 8 base
  samples; the grid needs several runs per cell before a region means anything. Every interval the
  reports print says so, and a run on a quieter machine would be the first thing to redo.
- **`NO_BAND_MERGER` is uninformative at these seeds**: bands merge in no baseline replicate, so the
  arm had nothing to remove. The counter exists now and a longer or differently seeded batch would
  say whether the mechanism matters.
- **The five-line reading is where the arms separate.** The declared six-line breakdown fires in
  almost nothing; `line_sensitivity` shows the baseline at 4–5 lines and the migration arm at 6. A
  later phase should decide whether the line or the mechanisms are mis-scaled — that is a reading
  rule and a fixture-scale question, not a parameter to fit.
- **Trade disruption has no endogenous driver.** The baseline's regime is a declared constant, so
  `NO_TRADE_DISRUPTION` measures the removal of a scenario assumption. The band→transport link is
  named in the code (`MarketClearingSystem.set_disruption`) and still unimplemented.
- **Full military pay raises arrears.** The finding is coherent (the claim exceeds any month's
  silver) but it is one model's answer; the mechanism deserves a dedicated experiment in P13 rather
  than a footnote here.
- **The market-connectivity measures are new and unvalidated.** They are defined in
  `analysis/outcomes.py`, printed everywhere, and have never been compared against anything.
