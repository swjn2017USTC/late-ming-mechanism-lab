# P04 — Market / Credit / Local Elite

Date: 2026-09-12
Status: complete. No P05 work started.

## Scope

Give the households a real counterparty: a county grain market with endogenous prices,
intercounty trade with declared transport cost and a violence-risk hook, aggregated merchant
houses, and elite estates that collect rent, lend, buy land, release relief and mediate tax. The
phase then measures four mechanism questions instead of asserting answers.

### Acceptance criteria

```text
county grain inventory · endogenous local grain price · intercounty trade · transport cost and
violence-risk placeholder · merchant layer · LocalEliteAgent · elite lending · debt servicing ·
land sale and purchase · private relief and grain release · tax mediation placeholder
interface · trade-network disruption hook · the four mechanism questions A-D · outputs: price
dispersion, land distribution, debt distribution · new invariants and regression seeds
```

### Non-goals (explicitly not built)

No taxation, no state relief, no armies, rebels or desertion, no labour market, no calibration, no
LLM. Elites are an aggregate per node: there is no elite-to-elite credit network, no elite
competition, and no political protection.

## Contract decisions

1. **Prices are endogenous; the price *rule* is an assumption.** `price = reference × (target
   cover / inventory)^elasticity`, bounded. The rule, the reference level, the target cover and
   the elasticity are grade `S` and named as sensitivity targets; nothing was tuned to produce a
   result.
2. **P02's dimensionless units are fixed by declaration.** One cost unit becomes silver per shi
   through `transport_cost_tael_per_cost_unit_per_shi`; one capacity unit becomes shi per month;
   zero capacity means autarky. These mappings are declared once, in `MarketParameters`.
3. **Transport risk is a real loss, not a fee.** A consignment leaves the granary whole; the
   fraction destroyed by link risk never arrives, never earns silver, and is logged as its own
   `TRADE_LOSS` event, so a loss can never hide inside a shipment total. Transport *cost*, by
   contrast, is a decision hurdle: it must be beaten before a merchant ships, but P04 has no
   carrier actor, so no silver leaves anyone to pay it. The cost is recorded on every flow, and a
   phase that models carriers must move it into the ledger.
4. **Clearing order is declared.** Merchants arbitrage first with the silver they hold, then buy
   the local harvest surplus. Buying locally first spent the silver that importing needed, which
   is a cash-management artefact rather than a mechanism.
5. **Every transfer is written twice.** Both sides log their own event, and the invariants
   reconcile them: grain sold is grain bought, silver paid is silver received, land sold is land
   bought. This is what caught P03's hidden flow and what caught one in P04 (below).
6. **Elite claims are derived, never stored.** The outstanding claim on a node's households *is*
   the sum of their debts, so accrued interest cannot drift between borrower and lender records.
7. **Elites are rules, not verdicts.** Lending, land purchase, relief and mediation are declared
   rules with parameters. The phase forbids itself the sentence "the gentry were predatory":
   the same lending rule both delays collapse and enlarges the claim, and only measurement
   decides which dominates in a configuration.
8. **Tax mediation is an interface with no demand behind it yet.** Elites can advance an
   obligation, but no tax demand exists before P05, so the default demand is zero and nothing is
   advanced. The mechanism is exercised by a test that supplies a demand, so it is not a stub.
9. **Relief is measured, not moralised.** Elites release grain to cohorts whose trailing unmet
   share crosses a threshold, in proportion to measured distress, capped by what they hold above
   a declared carry-over.
10. **The P03 placeholders are deleted, not deprecated.** `distress_grain_price_tael_per_shi`,
    `grain_reference_price_tael_per_shi`, `land_distress_price_tael_per_mu`, `loan_to_value` and
    `interest_rate_monthly` are gone from `HouseholdParameters`, and a test asserts they cannot
    return. Rent, which P03 paid to nobody, now reaches the local elite's granary.
11. **One assembly, two experiments.** `build_toy_economy` wires the tick order once, so the P03
    shock experiment and the P04 mechanism experiments cannot drift apart in how they build the
    model.

## Defect found and fixed during the phase

The P04 invariant suite caught a real gap of the same class as P03's blocker: `EliteActionSystem`
called `cohort.record_relief(...)` and `cohort.record_tax_mediation(...)` without emitting the
matching events, so a cohort's grain and silver moved with no entry in the log. The
balance-reconciliation test failed with "`grain_shi` went negative at tick 11" when replaying the
log, because the log was missing inflows the live state had received. Fixed by emitting both
events; the invariant test now passes on runs where relief fires, and it is the guard against the
whole class.

## The four mechanism questions

Full 1625–1644 window, toy economy, one rule changed per question. Severity is a scenario
parameter throughout; none of this is calibrated.

### A — does integration reduce spatial price dispersion?

| scenario | mean max/min price ratio | shipped grain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| autarky (capacity 0×) | 1.79 | 0 |
| integrated (1×) | 2.93 | 5,438 |
| integrated (3×) | 2.34 | 3,950 |
| integrated (10×) | 2.31 | 3,990 |
| merchant stock 10× | 2.67 | 18,800 |
| merchant stock 100× | 1.00 | 0 |

**No, in this configuration it does not** — opening the links *widens* the spread, and only
pinning every node to the price floor (100× stock, nothing left to arbitrage) produces equality.
The mechanism is visible in the numbers: arbitrage drains the selling node, and with 900 shi of
merchant stock against 2,100 shi of monthly local need — a fourteenth of the declared six-month
cover — one consignment is a large share of that stock,
so the source's price rises faster than the destination's falls. This is a statement about this
price rule, this topology and these endowments.

### B — does higher transport cost reduce trade flow?

| cost multiplier | shipped shi | lost shi |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.5× | 6,470 | 626 |
| 1× | 5,438 | 515 |
| 2× | 2,728 | 216 |
| 4× | 0 | 0 |

**Yes, monotonically**, and the flow shuts off entirely once the cost exceeds the price gap.

### C — does credit delay household collapse?

| scenario | mean unmet share | first below-floor month | land sold (mu) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| severe shock, credit on | 0.2527 | 38.5 | 4,481 |
| severe shock, credit off | 0.2605 | 33.4 | 11,874 |

**Yes in this configuration**: credit moves the first subsistence failure about five months later,
lowers the unmet share slightly, and halves forced land sales. The effect is modest because the
lender's capacity is bounded by the *borrower's* collateral and its own silver — a household with
no land or goods can borrow almost nothing, which is why the landless remain the worst affected.

### D — can credit increase long-run land concentration?

| scenario | elite land share | cohort land Gini | land sold (mu) | total claims (tael) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| no credit | 0.2519 | 0.5262 | 11,874 | 31,032 |
| credit at 0.5%/month | 0.2192 | 0.4965 | 4,481 | 86,593 |
| credit at 2%/month | 0.2211 | 0.4986 | 4,916 | 2,600,700 |

**Not here: credit substitutes for distress sales and therefore *reduces* concentration.** A
dearer rate (0.5% → 2% a month) pushes concentration back up a little (0.2192 → 0.2211) and
multiplies the claims stock thirtyfold. That last number is a placeholder pathology worth stating plainly: P04 has no
bankruptcy, write-off or rescheduling rule, so an unserviceable loan compounds forever. It is
recorded as the first thing a credit market model must fix, not presented as a finding.

## Review

The phase was put through an independent read-only review before commit (verdict:
approve-with-followups; no blockers). It verified all twelve required items as functional rather
than nominal, confirmed that double entry is structural (`LedgerAgent._apply` is the only
movement path, atomic and non-negativity-checked), reproduced every number in the tables above,
and confirmed the absence of anger scalars, moral verdicts about elites, and out-of-scope
mechanisms. Its findings were all fixed before commit:

- **MAJOR:** the report labelled the dear-credit row "3%/month" while the experiment ran 2%; the
  integration test used 3%. The test and the experiment now both use 2%, the table is labelled
  2%, and the numbers above are the 2% run's.
- rent was still logged as paid to an "unmodelled landlord" although it now reaches the elite:
  the household's rent event now names the counterparty (`paid-to:<elite>`), and the four
  regression digests were re-pinned for the change.
- the intercounty leg had no reconciliation test: a new invariant asserts that what arrives is
  what the buyer receives and pays, that the destroyed fraction is logged on the exporter's side,
  and that the risk mechanism actually destroys something in a shocked run.
- transport cost is now documented once as a hurdle rather than a payment (contract decision 3).
- the thin-stock arithmetic was corrected (900 shi against 2,100 shi of monthly need is a
  fourteenth of the six-month cover, not a tenth).
- the elite state snapshot got its own rule version instead of borrowing the relief one.

## Open items for P05 and later

- **The price rule is the weakest link.** A fixed target cover with no demand elasticity cannot
  equalise prices under arbitrage, and it pins to the floor or the ceiling whenever inventory is
  far from target. P08/P09 should replace it with a rule whose parameters are sourced, and P12
  should test the alternatives.
- **Credit has no bankruptcy, default or rescheduling.** Claims compound without limit. Until
  that is fixed, debt *levels* in long runs are not interpretable; ratios and directions are.
- **No labour market.** The in-kind wage still follows the last local harvest as a declared
  placeholder, which is why the landless track employment rather than wages.
- **Elites are one aggregate per node.** No elite-to-elite credit network, no competition, no
  political protection, and "hide taxable resources" is deferred to P05 with the tax system.
- **Tax mediation has no demand behind it.** The interface exists and is tested with a supplied
  demand; P05 supplies the real one and must decide what an advance costs the household.
- **Trade topology is still declared, not derived.** The arbitrage result depends on which links
  exist, and the toy links are not geography.
- **Merchant endowments are arbitrary.** Question A's answer moved with the stock scale, so the
  first thing P08 should source is how much grain a county's merchants actually held.
