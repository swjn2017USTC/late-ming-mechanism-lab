# P13 - Mechanism Discovery / Mechanism Cards

Date: 2026-09-14
Status: complete. No P14 work started.

## Scope

Read everything P08-P12 produced - the evidence registry, the calibration ensemble and its
predictive pass, the Morris and Sobol screens, the ablations, the counterfactual arms and the
policy-robustness matrix - and write down what the model's behaviour supports as a *mechanism*: six
candidates, one card each, with the brief's sixteen fields, a status from a five-word vocabulary, and
every number traceable to the batch it came from. Then describe the shape of the run tables with a
small fitted boundary, without letting that description stand in for an intervention.

Non-goals, stated in the scope and held to: no new simulation subsystem, no mechanism change, no
parameter tuning, no re-fitting of the calibration, no historical outcome encoded anywhere, and no
claim of causal historical truth.

### Acceptance criteria

```text
docs/mechanisms/cards.yaml (machine-readable) · docs/mechanisms/M00N-*.md (human-readable) ·
docs/mechanisms/index.md · outputs/reports/mechanism-synthesis.md · sixteen fields per card ·
statuses from the five-word vocabulary only · no vague multi-factor claims ·
a fitted boundary section that describes and does not explain · independent review · commit
```

runtime arm, because no runtime arm ran - P12's refusal is recorded and nothing is imputed for it.

## What the phase answered

1. **Is the extraction inversion real?** Not as a property of the apparatus. It appears exactly when
   a decision rule reacts to a shortfall by pressing effort to its ceiling, and it is absent when the
   rule holds. The card is CONDITIONAL, and the only arm that varies extraction collects *more* of
   the quota with escalation present - the opposite of the inversion story.
2. **Is the crisis gated?** Yes, and by movement rather than by climate. Opening the mobility gate
   breaks the county in every replicate, at the same tick, with the tax base down 162,553 mu; the
   drought's own effects are departures (-534 in the no-drought arm) and a *higher* arrears stock.
   The card is SUPPORTED on an intervention, not on a correlation.
3. **Does the fiscal-military ratchet hold?** No, in its stated form. Arrears rise from 254 tael to
   14-27 thousand against ~300 thousand assessed, and decline in 11-53 months of every 240-tick run.
   What survives is a level claim: the stock is chronically large and never clears within the
   window's closure, which the grade-A pattern `pay-monetised-and-arrears` matches at 1.00.
4. **Does elite mediation bifurcate?** The evidence resolves one branch and not the pair, so the
   card is WEAK rather than CONDITIONAL. Credit is load-bearing - removing it deepens the contraction
   by 9,540 mu and pushes 121 more households out per run - while the land-transfer pattern is
   satisfied at 0.94 in the same runs that the base contracts, so mediation and accumulation co-occur
   rather than forming the two branches the mechanism names.
5. **Does consolidation depend on the decision policy?** No: it is present in five of six replicates
   under each of the three declared policies. What the phase cannot claim is the chain: the merger
   rule's cohesion bar never fired anywhere in P10, so consolidation here is produced by formation
   and dissolution, not by the link the model documents.
6. **What does the model not measure at all?** Mortality. Famine mortality is the record's own link
   between harvest failure and population loss, it has no rule, no parameter and no output, and the
   abandonment pattern it is supposed to help produce is satisfied at 1.00 without it. That card is
   UNIDENTIFIED, and its counterexample says why pattern agreement was never evidence for it.

## The boundary analysis, and what it is not

`analysis/boundary.py` fits what the run tables can carry: on the P12 grid (54 runs, two scenario
knobs, logistic) the cross-validated AUC is 0.754 against an in-sample 0.723, with severity weighing
more than pressure (+0.73 against +0.33) and partial dependence rising 0.404 -> 0.462 -> 0.579 across
the three pressure levels; per policy the same fit gives 0.725 (rule), 0.800 (utility), 0.550
(random) on identical knobs, which is the region's movement seen through coefficients rather than
cells. On the Morris table (32 runs, 30 crossing the line) the fit is **refused**, not forced: the
declared five-fold split cannot be scored, and dropping to a split the table can carry would produce
a number that measures the fold split, so no parameter-side boundary is published and the parameter
statement stays with the Morris screen. `CAUSAL_DISCLAIMER` is rendered verbatim in the same section,
and the report says there what the surfaces cannot do: they describe the runs that were made; the
ablation arms are what carry causal weight in this phase.

## Design decisions worth recording

- **The schema refuses vagueness.** `VAGUE_PHRASES` is checked over every prose field, and a
  `SUPPORTED` card must cite at least two evidence kinds, one of them an intervention. A card cannot
  be written as "many factors interacted" and it cannot be supported by watched quantities alone.
- **Numbers are read, not transcribed.** Every card field is built from `synthesis/evidence.py`
  accessors over the real artifacts, so a card cannot drift from the batch it cites. The integration
  test loads the YAML back and compares it with the objects, and checks every citation against the
  filesystem or the source registry.
- **The sixth card.** The brief said at least five and not to assume all five hold. The five are
  assessed; the sixth is the gap the evidence named. A mechanism the project cannot measure is
  recorded as `UNIDENTIFIED` rather than omitted or folded into a neighbour.
- **ML as description.** The boundary module is standard-library-plus-sklearn, seeded, refuses a
  constant or single-class target, and never touches the ablation path. `report.py` is the only
  caller, and it renders the disclaimer where the fit is quoted.

## Independent review

`P13Review` (read-only) recomputed the cards' numbers against the batches with polars, re-rendered
the generated documents into a temporary directory and diffed them, and checked each status against
its own rules. Verdict: **approve with follow-ups** - "every ablation effect, calibration verdict,
pattern share, P12 presence/verdict and grid count I recomputed matches the shipped cards, and the
generated docs are byte-identical to what the current code renders". It found no fabricated number,
but it found prose that reached past its artifact, and all of it was fixed:

| finding | what was wrong | what the phase did |
|---|---|---|
| M005 sensitivity ranking | named the wrong top three for the largest band share, contradicting the card's own citation | the trio is now read from the Morris table in the bundle, and the card says the two rankings share one leader rather than three |
| M003 largest end stock | claimed the largest-stock replicate had the most declines; it is 26,849 tael with 37, not 26,488 with 53 | the figure is now taken from the reading text of the replicate that actually has the largest end stock |
| M003 decline range | the report generalised the rule arm's 11-53 months to all 18 replicates | the report now quotes the full range across the three policies, computed from the readings |
| M003 time lag | asserted when the first decline falls, which no artifact records | rewritten: the reading records how many months declined, not which ones |
| M002 counterexample | "the fifteen other arms" - there are twelve arms and ten besides the two named | rewritten, and the sentence now also records that breakdown does occur elsewhere in P10 (7 of 40 Sobol runs, one tipping cell) |
| M004 status | CONDITIONAL overstated what the evidence licenses for a bifurcation nobody measured | moved to WEAK, with the resolved half (mediation load-bearing) stated explicitly and the bifurcation's two-branch claim left open |
| M001/M002 sensitivity | "third in the ranking" hid a tie at mu* = 0.75 | the tie is stated in both cards |
| M002 causal chain | "an order of magnitude more" - the measured factor is 7x by median difference | now quotes the factor and the statistic |
| failed pattern cited as support | `chongzhen-drought-sequence` (held-out 0.00) was listed under historical support | a `historical-challenge` evidence kind was added, and the failed check is cited under it |
| M002 policy robustness | transferred a P10 gate result to all three P12 policies | rewritten: the cells move with the policy; that the gate carries the breakdown was tested under one policy |
| boundary note (4.2) | slid from coefficients to "the policy shaping" the outcome | rewritten as description, with the random arm's 0.550 +/- 0.400 named as a fit that separates almost nothing |
| index one-liner | split on "." and cut "rule 0.83" into "rule 0" | cuts on ". " so decimals survive |
| M005 trigger and chain | stated the band count rising (unresolved) and grain demands exceeding collection (unmeasured) | the first is marked unresolved, the second attributed to the record rather than to the model |

Findings the review could not verify are recorded in its own output as such: M004's claim about
monthly consumption events, M006's structure claim about the model's events, and the falsifiable
predictions, which describe arms that do not exist yet - predictions by construction.

## Changes to earlier phases

- **No mechanism, parameter, rule or tick-order change.** The only code outside `synthesis/` and
  `analysis/` is the new `analysis/boundary.py`; nothing in `systems/`, `actors/`, `networks/`,
  `policies/` or `calibration/` was touched.
- P12's mechanism readings are reused as the policy-robustness evidence rather than recomputed.
- `write_synthesis` gained an `artifacts_root` argument so a caller can write the report into a
  temporary directory while still reading the real batches; the default behaviour is unchanged.
